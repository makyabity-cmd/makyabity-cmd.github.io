Earth Temple walkthrough

Explored together with Medli, awakened as the Sage of Earth. The puzzles revolve around reflecting light with Medli's harp or the Mirror Shield to destroy statues and reveal transparent chests. Three floors: 1F, B1F and B2F.

Location: Headstone Island (C7) · Story chapter 11

How to enter: Learn the Ballad of Gales, get the Fire & Ice Arrows on the Mother and Child Isles and the Power Bracelets on Fire Mountain, then learn the Earth God's Lyric on Headstone Island. Warp to Dragon Roost Island with the Ballad of Gales, leave the Rito Aerie through the back door on 2F and go right. Cross the gap with the Grappling Hook, climb the ladder, raise the Wind Waker in front of Medli practicing her harp on the cliff and play the Earth God's Lyric to awaken her as the Sage of Earth. Medli waits on the King of Red Lions, so return to Headstone Island (warp to Outset and sail one square east), stand Medli beside you at the stone tablet in the cave and play the Earth God's Lyric; she plays along, the tablet crumbles and the entrance opens.

Puzzle tips

  • Press R to have Medli follow you or wait. Pick her up and jump to fly a long way.
  • You can control Medli with the Command Melody. Press A while light is hitting her to switch back to Link with the reflection angle locked.
  • Light petrifies black ChuChus (smash them with the Skull Hammer) and makes Poes solid. Transparent chests appear when you shine light on their outline.
  • Floormasters drag you back to set spots. If one grabs Medli, attack right away so she isn't taken.

Chests & items (22)

  1. Preparation · Ballad of Gales
    Shoot Cyclos in the cyclone at sea three times with the Hero's Bow before you're swallowed.
  2. Preparation · Fire & Ice Arrows
    After Forsaken Fortress 2, warp to the Mother and Child Isles with the Ballad of Gales and get them from the Queen of Fairies.
  3. Preparation · Power Bracelets
    Shoot an Ice Arrow into the crater at the top of Fire Mountain to freeze it for 5 minutes. Drop in through the hole at the top in the meantime and defeat every enemy inside for the chest.
  4. Preparation · Earth God's Lyric
    Lift the stone head at the Headstone Island cave entrance with the Power Bracelets and learn it from the stone tablet inside.
  5. 1F · Dungeon Map
    Past the room where you defeat all enemies. Shine light from Medli on the outline of the transparent chest to reveal it.
  6. 1F · Joy Pendant (behind the curtain)
    Burn the curtain in the warp-pot room with a Fire Arrow and grab the pendant behind it with the Boomerang or Hookshot.
  7. 1F · Small Key
    South of the central device room. Pull the block at the top of the right ladder toward you to let light in, then shine it on the transparent chest with Medli.
  8. 1F · Compass
    Past the north room. Shine light on the yellow patterns on the wall and set all the blocks that appear into the grooves in the floor for the chest.
  9. 1F · Joy Pendant
    Up the stairs that appear after you defeat two Moblins and two Poes in the south room. Shine light on the statue to destroy it.
  10. 1F · Small Key (coffin)
    Inside one of the five coffins in the lower right room (not a chest).
  11. 1F · Mirror Shield
    Up the stairs that appear after you defeat the mini-boss, three Stalfos.
  12. 1F · Joy Pendant
    After getting the Mirror Shield, return to the west room. Put Medli on the switch and reflect light onto the wall with the Mirror Shield; it breaks, revealing the chest.
  13. B1F · Joy Pendant (behind the curtain)
    Burn the curtain next to the Skull Hammer switch with a Fire Arrow and grab the pendant behind it with the Boomerang or Hookshot.
  14. B1F · Small Key
    Chest at the back of the left room, through the blue smoke.
  15. B1F · Treasure Chart 20
    Chest that appears when you defeat all the Floormasters in the same left room.
  16. B1F · 20 Rupees
    Chest straight ahead past the door that appears when you destroy the statue with light.
  17. B2F · Purple Rupee
    Chest on the right side of the room full of mirrors.
  18. B2F · Joy Pendant
    Chest on the left side of the room full of mirrors (move a block with the Power Bracelets and shine light on it).
  19. B2F · Treasure Chart 12
    Hidden room revealed by lighting the wall symbol from the left pedestal. Light every coffin and defeat the three Stalfos that come out.
  20. B2F · Big Key
    Room past the north door. At the very back after defeating the Bubbles and Darknut.
  21. Boss · Boss defeated
    Defeat Jalhalla. Can be checked separately from the Heart Container.
  22. B1F · Heart Container
    Defeat the boss, Jalhalla.

Walkthrough

  1. Preparation · Before entering the temple
    1) Shoot Cyclos in the cyclone three times with the bow for the Ballad of Gales. 2) Warp to the Mother and Child Isles for the Fire & Ice Arrows (play the Ballad of Gales on your boat to leave). 3) At Fire Mountain, freeze the crater with an Ice Arrow and within 5 minutes drop in through the hole at the top and defeat every enemy for the Power Bracelets. 4) Move the stone head on Headstone Island and learn the Earth God's Lyric at the tablet in the cave. 5) Warp to Dragon Roost Island; go out the back door on 2F of the Rito Aerie, head right, cross the gap with the Grappling Hook and climb the ladder to the cliff where Medli practices her harp, and play her the Earth God's Lyric. 6) Medli waits on the boat; return to Headstone Island (warp to Outset and sail east), stand Medli by you at the tablet in the cave and play the Earth God's Lyric. The tablet crumbles and the entrance opens.
    Get: Ballad of Gales, Fire & Ice Arrows, Power Bracelets, Earth God's Lyric
  2. 1F · Medli and the pillar switch
    Carry Medli and jump to the far side. In the room past the stairs, carry Medli onto the northeast pillar, stand on the switch yourself and use the Command Melody to fly her to the switch on the southeast pillar; the door opens.
  3. 1F · Map from the transparent chest
    Defeat all the enemies to open the door. Lure the black ChuChus into the light, or petrify them with Medli's light and smash them with the Skull Hammer. Shine Medli's light on the outline of the transparent chest for the map.
    Get: Dungeon Map, Joy Pendant (behind the curtain)
  4. 1F · Clear the smoke and get the Small Key in the south room
    Shine Medli's light on the smoke to the right of the central device to clear it and pound the switch that appears with the Skull Hammer; the south door opens. In the south room, climb the right ladder and pull the block toward you to let light in, then reveal the transparent chest with Medli.
    Get: Small Key
  5. 1F · Compass in the north room
    Shoot a Fire Arrow at the banner leaking light to burn it. Petrify a black ChuChu with light, carry it onto the switch and stairs appear (they retract after a while, so hurry). Drop the large block above to open the door. In the next room, move the near block to let light in and shine it on the yellow pattern on the wall to make a warp pot and blocks appear. Destroy the statue on the block with light and set all the blocks into the grooves for the Compass.
    Get: Compass
  6. 1F · South room, pendant and the coffin key
    Defeat two Moblins and two Poes to make stairs appear (make the Poes solid with light, then slash). Destroy the statue above with light for a pendant. Check the five coffins in the lower right room; one has the Small Key (the rest have ReDeads).
    Get: Joy Pendant, Small Key (coffin)
  7. 1F · Mini-boss: Stalfos → Mirror Shield
    Open the south door with the Small Key. See “Mini-boss” below. Defeat all three and stairs appear to the Mirror Shield. Reflect the light in the middle of the room with the Mirror Shield onto the sun symbol above the door to open it.
    Get: Mirror Shield
  8. 1F · Shine light into both eyes of the device
    Back in the central room, have Medli shine light into one eye of the device and lock it with A, then as Link reflect light into the other eye with the Mirror Shield; the mist clears and stairs to the basement appear.
    Get: Joy Pendant
  9. B1F · Connect the light in the central room
    Cross the rope bridge with Medli and play the Earth God's Lyric at the tablet. Reflect the light coming into the center off the mirrors on the left and right and the statue on the central block.
  10. B1F · Aim the mirror in the right room
    Two Floormasters lurk in the mist. Throw a bomb just before it explodes to clear the mist. Pound the switch at the back with the Skull Hammer to let light in, then push the block with the mirror into the beam.
    Get: Joy Pendant (behind the curtain)
  11. B1F · Small Key and Treasure Chart 20 in the left room
    Go through the blue smoke for the Small Key in the chest at the back (taking it clears the smoke). Defeat all the Floormasters for Treasure Chart 20.
    Get: Small Key, Treasure Chart 20
  12. B1F · From the Poe room to the tablet
    Push the central block to climb the high ground and go to the locked door. Make the Poes solid with light and defeat them. Reflect light with Medli and Link to destroy the lower left statue and reveal a door. The chest straight ahead has 20 Rupees. Coffin lids fall over, so don't stand too close. Play the Earth God's Lyric at the north tablet.
    Get: 20 Rupees, et_tstatue
  13. B2F · Open the warp pot
    Carry Medli and jump across. Opening the warp pot near the entrance with a bomb makes things easier.
  14. B2F · Build a path for the light
    Throw Medli onto the central switch to release the light. On each side, reflect light from the pedestal to destroy the central statue, move the statue that appears, and shine light on the wall symbol to reveal a hidden statue. Finally stand Medli on the left pedestal and Link on the right, and shine light into both eyes of the statue above at once to open the north door.
    Get: Purple Rupee, Joy Pendant
  15. B2F · (Optional) Treasure Chart 12
    Light the wall symbol from the left pedestal to reveal a hidden room. Light every coffin and defeat the three Stalfos for the chest.
    Get: Treasure Chart 12
  16. B2F · Big Key in the back room
    Bubbles and a Darknut appear. Use the parry attack on the Darknut; fan the Bubbles with the Deku Leaf, then slash. Defeat them all for the Big Key at the very back.
    Get: Big Key
  17. B1F · To the boss room
    In the spiral room linking B1F and B2F, use the Command Melody to fly Medli to B1F while Link climbs the vines on the wall. Carry Medli and fly straight ahead to reach the boss door.
  18. B1F · Boss: Jalhalla
    See “Boss” below
    Get: Boss defeated, Heart Container
  19. After clearing · On to the Wind Temple
    Take the Heart Container. Next is the Wind Temple.

Mini-boss: Stalfos (three)

  1. After a few hits its body falls apart; attack the head while it's in pieces.
  2. Defeating one brings two more out of the coffins. Fight them one at a time so you don't get surrounded.

Boss: Jalhalla (giant Poe)

  1. Reflect the light shining into the room onto it with the Mirror Shield; it freezes and becomes solid.
  2. Once it's solid, pick it up and throw it into the spiked pillars on the wall; it splits into small Poes.
  3. Defeat all the scattered small Poes to damage it. Repeat.
