Ganon's Tower walkthrough

The final dungeon. There's no Dungeon Map or Compass. Behind the four doors of the central hall you refight four earlier bosses; past the north door you get the Light Arrows in the underground maze, then face Puppet Ganon and Ganondorf back to back.

Location: Tower of the Gods (E4) · Story chapter 14

How to enter: Collect all eight Triforce Shards and go to the Tower of the Gods; a ring of light appears. Enter it to reach Hyrule Castle. After the scene in the Master Sword chamber, defeat the two Darknuts and go outside. Follow the path where the barrier has vanished to reach Ganon's Tower (Hookshot across in front of the cave).

Puzzle tips

  • In the boss refights you can only use the items you had when you first beat that boss.
  • Take pictos of any bosses you missed here (Gohma, Kalle Demos, Jalhalla, Molgera, Phantom Ganon, Puppet Ganon).
  • Light Arrows use magic but kill most enemies in one hit.
  • Opening the warp past the north door links to the Forsaken Fortress harbor so you can go out to restock.

Chests & items (2)

  1. Underground maze · Light Arrows
    Chest that appears when you defeat Phantom Ganon at the end of the underground maze.
  2. Top · Ganondorf defeated (final boss)
    After defeating Puppet Ganon, defeat Ganondorf at the top of the tower.

Walkthrough

  1. Before entering · To Ganon's Tower
    Complete the Triforce and go to the Tower of the Gods. Take the ring of light to Hyrule Castle and watch the scene in the Master Sword chamber. Defeat the two Darknuts, leave the castle and follow the path to Ganon's Tower. Hookshot across in front of the cave.
  2. Central hall · Take the four doors in any order
    Front left = Gohma, back left = Kalle Demos, front right = Jalhalla, back right = Molgera. Defeat all four to open the north door.
  3. Gohma's door · Cross the lava
    From the floating platforms on the lava, grapple the branches and glide across with the Deku Leaf. Hang from the far branch, wait for the swinging to stop, climb on top and glide to the platform with the Bubbles. The boss works as before: swing on Valoo's tail on the ceiling to crack the shell, hook the eye and slash.
  4. Kalle Demos's door · Ride two lifts
    Turn the left propeller with the Deku Leaf to call the lift and fan from the direction you came to move. From the moving platform, turn the propeller below to move the second lift, jump on with the Deku Leaf and do the same. When the moving platform is at its highest, glide to the boss room. The boss works as before: cut the vines with the Boomerang to drop it and slash the center.
  5. Jalhalla's door · Raise the stairs with petrified black ChuChus
    Use the Mirror Shield where light shines in to petrify a black ChuChu and put it on the switch; stairs appear (they retract when it thaws, so roll to hurry). The second staircase works the same. If Stalfos appear, defeat them and try again. The boss works as before: make it solid with light, throw it into the spiked pillars and defeat all the Poes it splits into.
  6. Molgera's door · Springboards and the Deku Leaf
    Step on the springboard in the Iron Boots, take them off midair and glide on with the Deku Leaf (defeat any Wizzrobes in the way first). Avoid the downdrafts, head to the row of pots and climb up using the Hookshot targets above. The boss works as before: Hookshot its tongue, pull it in and slash.
  7. Past the north door · Open the warp with the candle puzzle
    In the right candle room, count the candles on the walls (left to right: 2, 4, 1, 3). In the left room, hit the crystal switches with the Boomerang in order of fewest candles (2nd from right → far left → far right → 2nd from left). A warp appears in the waterway, linking to the Forsaken Fortress harbor.
  8. Underground maze · Choose the right doors
    Read the stone tablet in the candle rooms and a hole opens in the middle; drop in. Defeat the Phantom Ganon that appears and it drops a sword; the correct door is the one its hilt points to. With the fixed route, taking the way you face in the first room as forward: back → left → forward → left → right → forward. A wrong door leads to a room full of enemies.
  9. Underground maze · Get the Light Arrows and break the wall
    Defeat Phantom Ganon in the last room for the Light Arrows. Return to the first room through the far door; he appears again—defeat him with a Light Arrow, then pick up the black sword he leaves and strike the north wall to open the way.
    Get: Light Arrows
  10. Innermost area · Defeat every enemy and go to the big door
    Defeat all the Darknuts and Moblins to open the big door (Light Arrows kill them in one shot). Two boss fights in a row follow, so stock up on healing items.
  11. Innermost area · Mini-boss: Puppet Ganon
    See “Mini-boss” below
  12. Up the tower · Climb with ropes and the Hookshot
    Jump to the red rope hanging from the central platform, climb twice with the Grappling Hook and finally use the Hookshot target to reach the battlefield. On the way are pots with hearts and fairies, and a warp pot back to 1F.
  13. Top · Final boss: Ganondorf
    See “Boss” below
    Get: Ganondorf defeated (final boss)
  14. After clearing · About the Second Quest
    After the ending a Second Quest file is created automatically. If you want to keep collecting Pieces of Heart and figurines in your first file, save it to a separate slot.

Mini-boss: Puppet Ganon (three forms)

  1. Puppet form: cut the strings with the Boomerang (blue strings take two hits each; red ones can't be cut). Cutting the string holding the tail makes it hang down; shoot the blue ball on the tip with a Light Arrow. Three hits and it transforms.
  2. Spider form: spins on the ceiling and drops. Watch its reflection in the water on the floor to see where it lands and which way its tail points, then shoot the blue ball on the tail with a Light Arrow. Defeat the Keese quickly. Three hits and it transforms.
  3. Snake form: moves fast. An arrow to the head stops it briefly; get around to the tail and hit the blue ball with a Light Arrow. Three hits finish it.

Boss: Ganondorf

  1. In the first half Princess Zelda backs you up with Light Arrows. Slash Ganondorf when an arrow staggers him.
  2. While targeting, position yourself so Zelda is behind Ganondorf; her arrows hit more easily.
  3. While Zelda is down, fight with the parry attack (when the A button flashes while targeting).
  4. After Zelda gets up, she fires Light Arrows toward you. Keep Ganondorf targeted, hold up the Mirror Shield to deflect the arrows into him, then slash.
