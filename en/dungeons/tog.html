Tower of the Gods walkthrough

The tower that rises from the sea once the three pearls are placed. On 1F the water level rises and falls at intervals, which you use to float crates and cross bridges of light. On 2F you learn the Command Melody, and bringing three statues to the center lets you reach 3F.

Location: Tower of the Gods (E4) · Story chapter 8

How to enter: Place Din's Pearl on the Northern Triangle Island (D3), Farore's Pearl on the Eastern Triangle Island (F4) and Nayru's Pearl on the Southern Triangle Island (D5), and the Tower of the Gods rises from the sea (E4).

Puzzle tips

  • The 1F water level rises and falls regularly; where you can go depends on whether it's high or low.
  • Cracked walls can be broken with Bombs (or your boat's cannon).
  • Eye switches on walls are triggered by shooting them with the Hero's Bow, which you get partway through.
  • Wake statues with R so they follow you. Carry them over pits; if one falls, it's back in place when you return.

Walkthrough

  1. 1F · Map in the front right room
    When the water is low, go through the front right door. Blow up the cracked wall to the south for the map. For the easternmost wall on the north side, push a crate in front of it, wait for the water to rise and bomb the wall from the floating crate (pendant).
    Get: Dungeon Map, Joy Pendant (pot)
  2. 1F · Compass in the upper left room
    Blow up the cracked wall to get in and defeat all the Bubbles. Carry fire with a Boko Stick from a pot to the two torches near the lower left room for a pendant.
    Get: Compass, Joy Pendant
  3. 1F · Cross-shaped key piece in the upper right room
    Set an Armos statue on the glowing pedestal to open the door. Inside, go down and put a crate on the glowing pedestal; a bridge of light appears only when the water is low. Take the cross-shaped piece from the far side out of the room and set it in the hollow in the floor to the left to open the gate.
  4. 1F · Run across the crates for a Small Key
    Past the gate, set the two Armos statues on pedestals to open the door. Line up the crates, then when the water rises dash across them holding a lit Boko Stick and light the two torches on the far side.
    Get: Small Key
  5. 1F · Defeat the yellow ChuChu to stop the water
    Open the lower left door with the Small Key. Yellow ChuChus shock you if you touch them, so use Bombs or the Boomerang. Set the cross-shaped piece from the top of the light stairs into the hollow outside the room on the left to stop the water in the middle of 1F.
  6. 1F→2F · Avoid the Beamos and take the lift
    In the back center room, move while the Beamos's eye isn't facing you. Put Armos statues on two of the glowing pedestals and stand on the third to ride the lift to 2F.
  7. 2F · Carry the statue for the Command Melody
    Examine the stone tablet at the back of the right room and press R near the statue to wake it. Lead it through the narrow path without dropping it, carry it over the pits and bring it to the center. Learn the Command Melody from the stone tablet that appears; the left door opens.
    Get: Command Melody
  8. 2F · Use the Command Melody to move the statue across
    In the left rooms, cross by hooking the ceiling bars with the Grappling Hook (two rooms). Wake the statue, stand on the floor switch to keep the light bridge up and use the Command Melody to walk the statue across. Swing across yourself, carry the statue back one room and set it on the left floor switch to open the right gate.
  9. 2F · Mini-boss: Darknut
    Cut the strap on its back to remove its armor and hit its head to knock off the helmet. When the A button flashes while targeting, use the special parry attack (it won't trigger while you're holding up your shield). Defeat it for the Hero's Bow.
    Get: Hero's Bow
  10. 2F · Bring the statue out to the center
    Go back one room and shoot the eye switch in front with the bow to make a moving platform. Grapple over, carry the statue onto the platform and ride to the central room. The gate above opens.
  11. 2F · (Optional) Two Armos Knights
    Head right, shoot the eye switch from the lift to raise the platform and go south. Stop the Armos Knights by shooting their eyes, then throw a bomb into their open mouths.
    Get: Joy Pendant
  12. 2F · Small Key in the lift room
    Through the right door by the water. Ride the lift and shoot the lower eye switch to move the platform up. From the very top, shoot the eye switch above the entrance for a pendant chest too.
    Get: Small Key, Joy Pendant
  13. 2F · North by the scale
    Put two Armos statues on one side of the scale over the puddle, jump onto the raised side and go north. Open the door with the Small Key.
  14. 2F · Past the lasers to the third statue
    Glide over the lasers with the Deku Leaf from the block and wake the statue. Use the Command Melody to walk it through the lasers onto the glowing pedestal to shut them off. Carry the statue out of the room.
  15. 2F→3F · Carry the statue across the scale
    This time put three Armos statues on one side of the scale, then carry the statue across on the raised side. With all three statues in the center, a light appears and takes you to 3F.
  16. 2F · (Optional) Treasure Chart 30
    Go to the cracked wall at the lower left. Hold a bomb until it's about to explode on the hanging platform and throw it at the wall. Play the Wind's Requiem on the wind symbol in the room with the two Armos Knights for the chest.
    Get: Treasure Chart 30, tg_tstatue
  17. 1F · (Optional) Treasure Chart 6
    Once you have the bow, shoot the eye switch on the wall of the 1F upper left room and take the chest.
    Get: Treasure Chart 6
  18. 3F · Stop the lasers for the Big Key
    Stand on one of the three floor switches with an Armos statue on each of the others to stop the lasers. The Big Key is in the chest at the back. When the two Armos start moving, shoot their eyes with the bow, then slash the crystal on their back.
    Get: Big Key, Joy Pendant (pot)
  19. 4F · Climb the outer stairs
    Outside the tower, climb while destroying the blue Beamos heads with the bow. Pots before the boss room refill your arrows and bombs.
  20. 4F · Boss: Gohdan
    See “Boss” below
    Get: Boss defeated, Heart Container
  21. After clearing · To Hyrule Castle
    Take the Heart Container and step into the light that appears to reach the bell at the top. Ring the bell to descend to Hyrule Castle under the sea.

Boss: Gohdan (giant stone head and hands)

  1. Shoot the eyes in the palms of both hands with the Hero's Bow to make them fall. Knock down both hands.
  2. While both hands are down, shoot both eyes on the head; it drops and opens its mouth.
  3. Throw a bomb into the open mouth to damage it. Repeat.
  4. Hands → eyes → bomb in the mouth three times defeats it. Falling off the platform shocks you, so fight in the middle. If you run out of arrows or bombs, Gohdan's nose drops what you need.
