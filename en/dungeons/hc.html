Hyrule Castle walkthrough

The castle sealed beneath the sea. On the first visit, after the Tower of the Gods, you get the Master Sword; on the second, after Forsaken Fortress 2, you come with Tetra. There are no chests, but it's a key story location.

Location: Tower of the Gods (E4) · Story chapters 9–10

How to enter: After beating the Tower of the Gods boss, ring the bell at the top to travel to Hyrule Castle with the King of Red Lions. The second time you're taken there automatically after the Forsaken Fortress 2 events.

Puzzle tips

  • There are few chances to photograph the Darknut (cape) figurine, so take a picto here.
  • The second visit is the last chance to photograph Tetra. A picto of Tetra also gets you the six pirates' figurines at once.

Chests & items (1)

  1. Basement · Master Sword
    First visit. Found in the basement revealed by the courtyard puzzle.

Walkthrough

  1. Tower of the Gods · Ring the bell
    At the top of the Tower of the Gods, climb the ladder in front, hook the bell with the Grappling Hook and swing hard to ring it; you're taken to Hyrule Castle.
  2. 1F · Match the Triforce on the floor
    Enter the castle and go to the center of the courtyard. Move the stones in the middle of the room to match the Triforce pattern on the floor; the hero statue moves and stairs to the basement appear.
  3. Basement · Draw the Master Sword
    Get the Master Sword in the basement.
    Get: Master Sword
  4. 1F・2F・B1F · Defeat every enemy that starts moving
    Time starts flowing again and Moblins and Darknuts appear. Defeat every enemy on 1F, 2F and B1F to remove the barrier on the exit. Use the hearts and bombs the enemies drop.
  5. Outside · Escape through the light
    Ride the King of Red Lions into the light. Your next destination is the Forsaken Fortress (A1).
  6. 2nd · To the basement with Tetra
    You're brought here after Forsaken Fortress 2. Take Tetra's picto at the entrance. Go in with Tetra to the open basement stairs. After the scene, leave the castle and head for the Earth and Wind Temples.
