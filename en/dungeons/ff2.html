Forsaken Fortress (2nd visit) walkthrough

With the Master Sword, you return to rescue Aryll and the others. This time you have a sword and can fight normally. Defeat the mini-boss Phantom Ganon for the Skull Hammer, follow the same route as before to the tower and fight the Helmaroc King at the top.

Location: Forsaken Fortress (A1) · Story chapter 10

How to enter: The entrance is blocked by a wooden door. Equip Bombs, use them on the King of Red Lions and blast the door with your boat's cannon.

Puzzle tips

  • The Helmaroc King and Tetra (Hyrule Castle) can only be photographed around now. Bring the Deluxe Picto Box.
  • If a Floormaster grabs you, you're sent back to the 1F jail.
  • This time you can ignore the searchlights.

Chests & items (3)

  1. Courtyard · Skull Hammer
    Chest that appears when you defeat the mini-boss Phantom Ganon
  2. Boss · Boss defeated
    Defeat the Helmaroc King. Can be checked separately from the Heart Container.
  3. Rooftop · Heart Container
    Defeat the boss, the Helmaroc King.

Walkthrough

  1. Entrance · Break the door with your boat's cannon
    Use Bombs on the King of Red Lions to fire the cannon. Shoot the wooden door to break it and go in.
  2. Courtyard · Mini-boss: Phantom Ganon
    Appears when you approach the big door. See “Mini-boss” below. Defeat it for the Skull Hammer.
    Get: Skull Hammer
  3. 1F · Pound the stakes with the Skull Hammer
    Go into the fortress through the big door. Pound down any stakes in the way with the Skull Hammer. Go counterclockwise to the lower left room and climb the ladder on the bed to 2F.
    Get: Yellow Rupee
  4. 1F · (Optional) Piece of Heart
    If you missed it the first time, get the chest in the 1F jail cell (opened by the floor switch by the barrels). Linked to the Forsaken Fortress (1st visit) chest list.
    Get: Piece of Heart
  5. 2F · Glide across with the Deku Leaf and go outside
    2F is also counterclockwise. Where you swung across with a rope last time, glide with the Deku Leaf to the platform with the Bokoblin. Go outside through the big door in the upper left room.
  6. Outside · To Aryll's tower
    Same route as the first visit. If a searchlight catches you on the narrow ledges you get shelled, so defeat the Bokoblins first or cross quickly with the Deku Leaf or by grappling the branches above. Pound the stake in front of the tower door with the Skull Hammer to open it.
  7. Tower · Race up the spiral slope
    After the cutscene, water floods in and the Helmaroc King attacks. Run up the slope and grapple over the broken sections. When its face blocks the way, smash it with the Skull Hammer to knock it off (a safe moment to take its picto).
  8. Rooftop · Boss: Helmaroc King
    See “Boss” below
    Get: Boss defeated, Heart Container
  9. After clearing · Ganondorf, then Hyrule Castle
    Take the Heart Container and see the scene with Ganondorf in the building at the back. Valoo then carries you to Hyrule Castle.

Mini-boss: Phantom Ganon

  1. You can't hit it while it's in the air. When it fires an energy ball, bat it back with the Master Sword.
  2. It hits the ball back too, so keep rallying until it misses.
  3. When the ball hits, it falls to the ground and kneels; run in and slash it.
  4. Later it appears behind you and swings at you. Back off to dodge, or counter with the parry attack while targeting.

Boss: Helmaroc King (giant bird)

  1. Fought at the top of the tower. When it drives its beak into the floor, smash the mask on its face with the Skull Hammer.
  2. Once the mask breaks, hit its head with your sword when its beak is stuck in the floor, and with the Hero's Bow when it's stuck in the outer wall.
  3. Watch its charges and beak attacks carefully and dodge.
