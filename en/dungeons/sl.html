Savage Labyrinth walkthrough

A 50-floor labyrinth beneath Outset Island. Defeat every enemy on a floor to put out the fire over the hole to the next. Every 10 floors (B10, B20, B30, B40, B50) there's a rest area with rupees, healing pots and a column of light leading outside. Leaving sends you back to B1. Triforce Chart 6 is on B30, so you must visit at least once.

Location: Outset Island (B7) · Story chapter 13 (optional)

How to enter: Hookshot up the tree on the cliff behind Link's house on Outset Island, then lift the stone head at the end of the west cliff with the Power Bracelets to reveal the entrance.

Puzzle tips

  • Healing is scarce inside. Bring fairies, Grandma's Elixir Soup or Blue Potions in bottles.
  • It's easier after adding hearts and upgrading arrow/bomb capacity and magic at the Great Fairies.
  • In the later floors, Light Arrows kill Darknuts, Wizzrobes and Stalfos in one hit. Save magic for the floors you find hard.
  • Also good for farming rupees (go down to B40 and leave, repeat).

Walkthrough

  1. Entrance · Entering the labyrinth
    Hookshot up to the cliff behind Link's house and lift the stone head with the Power Bracelets.
  2. B1〜B9 · Early floors
    Keese, Miniblins, Bokoblins, red ChuChus, Magtails, Fire Keese, Moblins and so on—mostly enemies from Dragon Roost Cavern and the Forsaken Fortress.
  3. B10 · Rest area
    20 Rupees. Exit through the column of light.
    Get: 20 Rupees
  4. B11〜B19 · Forest enemy floors
    Peahats, green ChuChus, Boko Babas, Mothulas and so on. Knock the wings off the winged Mothula on B19 with the Boomerang, then slash. Spin-attack the Morths as a group.
  5. B20 · Rest area
    104 Rupees
    Get: 104 Rupees
  6. B21〜B29 · Tower enemy floors
    Wizzrobes, Armos, Armos Knights, yellow ChuChus, red Bubbles, Darknuts and so on. Circle to dodge the Wizzrobes on B21 and hit them as they finish warping. Stun high targets with the Boomerang, then use the bow.
  7. B30 · Triforce Chart 6
    Play the Wind's Requiem on the blue floor for the chest. There are also 202 Rupees. To go further, shine light on the statue with the Mirror Shield.
    Get: Triforce Chart 6, 202 Rupees
  8. B31〜B39 · Temple enemy floors
    ReDeads, blue Bubbles, black ChuChus, Poes, Mothulas, Stalfos and so on. Stop the ReDeads on B31 by reflecting the light from above onto them with the Mirror Shield.
  9. B40 · Rest area
    498 Rupees
    Get: 498 Rupees
  10. B41〜B49 · Tough floors
    B41 lots of Miniblins (gather them and spin attack), B42 lots of ChuChus (pound the floor with the Skull Hammer to stun them all), B43 lots of Wizzrobes, B44 lots of Bokoblins (dark—fight near the torches), B45 Stalfos + ReDeads (bomb the Stalfos apart, then deal with the ReDeads), B48 Stalfos (beware of Bomb Flowers on the floor setting each other off), B47 and B49 Darknuts (separate them or use Light Arrows).
  11. B50 · Bottom floor
    Yellow Rupee (10)
    Get: Piece of Heart
