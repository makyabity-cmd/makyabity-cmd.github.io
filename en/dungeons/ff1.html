Forsaken Fortress (1st visit) walkthrough

Your first infiltration, to rescue Aryll. You lose your sword as soon as you land, so sneak past the searchlights and Moblin guards. If you're spotted you're thrown in jail, but starting from the jail actually makes the route easier to follow.

Location: Forsaken Fortress (A1) · Story chapter 2

How to enter: Sail there on Tetra's pirate ship from Outset Island. After getting the Spoils Bag on the ship, climb the ladder in the middle of the deck and you're fired at the island from the catapult.

Puzzle tips

  • While you have no sword, pick up Boko Sticks from pots and use them as weapons.
  • Wear a barrel to move past guarded areas without being seen.
  • Shimmy along narrow ledges by pressing against the wall.

Chests & items (5)

  1. Pirate ship · Spoils Bag
    Chest across the gap: talk to the pirate in the blue bandana, press the switch and swing over on the rope.
  2. 2F · Dungeon Map
    Chest at the end of the path through the hole in the jail cell wall.
  3. 1F · Piece of Heart
    The 1F room directly below the Dungeon Map room. Step on the floor switch in the corner by the barrels to open the cell with the chest.
  4. 2F · Compass
    Chest between the barrels, to the right of the Dungeon Map room.
  5. 1F · Yellow Rupee
    Chest on the bed in a 1F room (also obtainable on your second visit).

Walkthrough

  1. Pirate ship · Get the Spoils Bag
    On the ship, talk to the pirate in the blue bandana and press the switch as told. Swing back and forth on the rope and jump across. If you fall, the switch by the ladder raises the platform again.
    Get: Spoils Bag
  2. Landing · Catapulted to the Forsaken Fortress
    Climb the ladder in the middle of the deck to be fired from the catapult. You lose your sword, so fight with Boko Sticks from pots from here on.
  3. Jail · Get caught on purpose and escape from the jail
    If a searchlight or Moblin spots you, you're thrown in jail. Jump from the table to the shelf by the wall, move the pot on top and crawl through the hole behind it.
  4. 2F · Dungeon Map
    Chest at the end of the path
    Get: Dungeon Map
  5. 1F · (Optional) Piece of Heart
    In the corner of the 1F room below the map room, step on the floor switch by the barrels to open the cell with the chest.
    Get: Piece of Heart
  6. 2F · Compass
    Chest between the barrels to the right of the map room
    Get: Compass
  7. Outside · Shut down the three searchlights
    Each stops when you defeat its Bokoblin. Get a stick from the pots nearby, or block a Bokoblin's attack with your shield so it drops its weapon. The first is up the ladder outside the left door of the map room, the second up the slope from the compass, the third up from near the compass.
  8. 2F · Sneak through the guard room in a barrel
    Moblins patrol the north room, so wear a barrel to reach the big door at the back. Slip past the other one outside in a barrel too. Shimmy across the two narrow ledges (if you fall, go back the same way).
  9. In front of the tower · Get your sword back and reach Aryll's room
    Pick up the Hero's Sword in front of the big door to start a fight with shield-carrying Bokoblins. Defeat them to open the door and find Aryll. After the cutscene you end up on Windfall.
