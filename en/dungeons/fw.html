Forbidden Woods walkthrough

The dungeon deep in Forest Haven, from B1F to 4F. You mostly launch high from Baba Buds (purple flowers) and glide with the Deku Leaf. First climb to 4F without the Boomerang, beat the mini-boss Mothula for the Boomerang, then cut through vines on doors and ceilings on the way down to B1F for the Big Key.

Location: Forest Haven (F6) · Story chapter 6

How to enter: After getting the Deku Leaf inside Forest Haven, refill your magic and go outside. Set the wind southwest and glide to the islet to the southwest, then set the wind northwest and ride the updraft over the sea to the Forbidden Woods.

Puzzle tips

  • Throw seeds or Bomb Flowers at the plants blocking doors to destroy them. Throw from a bit away so the flower doesn't close its mouth.
  • Lifts and red flowers move when you fan them with the Deku Leaf from the opposite side.
  • Defeated Boko Babas sometimes turn into Baba Buds (purple flowers you can launch from).

Walkthrough

  1. 1F · Get the map and open the door with a seed
    Map in the chest at the back right. Pick up the seed on the left and throw it from a little way off at the plant on the front door.
    Get: Dungeon Map
  2. B1F→2F · Launch to 2F from a Baba Bud
    Launch from the Baba Bud on B1F and glide to 2F with the Deku Leaf. Blow up the plant on the door with a nearby Bomb Flower.
    Get: Knight's Crest
  3. 2F · Move the lift with the pinwheel switch
    Fan the left pinwheel with the Deku Leaf to bring the lift over. Once on, fan from the opposite side of where you want to go. Of the three Boko Babas in the next room, defeat the rightmost one to make a Baba Bud and go up to 3F.
    Get: Joy Pendant (pot), Red Rupee
  4. 3F · Carry a seed on the lift
    Bring the lift over with the pinwheel and go to the south platform. Break the barricade with your sword, take the seed, carry it north on the lift and throw it at the plant on the door.
  5. 3F · Blow away the seed near the thorns
    Thorns pop up around the seed in the middle, so blow it away with the Deku Leaf from a distance, pick it up and throw it at the plant on the far door.
  6. 3F · Ride the moving branches
    Grapple the right branch to cross. Ride the moving branches counterclockwise and carry the seed from the north platform to the door on the west platform.
    Get: Yellow Rupee
  7. 3F · Thorny maze
    Thorns spring up as you get close, so walk slowly. Blow up the two barricades with the Bomb Flower to the south (throw at the west one from just outside thorn range). Compass in the south, Small Key at the back. On the way back, launch over the maze from the nearby Baba Bud.
    Get: Compass, Small Key, Joy Pendant (pot)
  8. 3F→4F · Through the north door
    Open the north door with the Small Key and go on with the pinwheel and lift. Turn a Boko Baba into a Baba Bud and go up to 4F.
    Get: Joy Pendant
  9. 4F · Mini-boss: Mothula
    When it flies, knock it down with a gust from the Deku Leaf and slash it. After a few hits its wings come off and it gets easier. If Morths from its tail cling to you, use a spin attack. Defeat it for the Boomerang.
    Get: Boomerang
  10. 4F · Both crystals at once
    Lock onto both crystal switches above the door and throw the Boomerang to open it.
  11. 4F · Knock down the ceiling seed and cross
    Return to the previous room and cut the plant on the south door with the Boomerang. In the next room, cut down the seed hanging from the ceiling with the Boomerang, then glide straight ahead with the Deku Leaf.
    Get: Joy Pendant, fw_tstatue
  12. 4F→B1F · Drop the giant flower
    Cut the five vines holding the giant flower in the middle of the room with the Boomerang; it falls and opens a hole down to B1F.
  13. B1F · Red flower room
    First use the red flowers as platforms to grab the chest at the back (Yellow Rupee). Then cut the four vines to drop the flowers into the water and blow up the north barricade with the Bomb Flower to the south.
    Get: Yellow Rupee
  14. B1F · Treasure Chart 15 inside the stump
    Climb the platforms, turn a Boko Baba into a Baba Bud and launch up. Use the pinwheel and lift to reach the platform with the Bomb Flower and drop it into the hole in the central stump. Go into the hole, open the chest and crawl out through the passage behind it.
    Get: Treasure Chart 15
  15. B1F · Cross the water on red flowers
    Stand on a red flower floating in the water and fan it with the Deku Leaf from the opposite side of where you want to go. Dodge the Octorok rocks or stun them with the Boomerang.
  16. B1F · Five crystals for the Big Key
    From the top of the central platform, lock onto all five crystal switches around the room and throw the Boomerang. The Big Key is in the chest in the small room. Stun the two Moblins that appear right after with the Boomerang and attack. Hook the ceiling branch from the platform to reach the exit.
    Get: Big Key
  17. B1F→2F · Ride the updraft to 2F
    Back in the room you dropped into with the giant flower, spin the pinwheel left of the door with the Deku Leaf to create an updraft. Launch from the Baba Bud and glide to 2F. Cut the plant on the right door with the Boomerang.
  18. 2F · Two Mothulas
    Defeating them opens the door and makes a pendant chest appear.
    Get: Joy Pendant
  19. Before the boss · Warp pot and fairies
    Light the torch with a Boko Stick to open the warp pot before the boss room; it links to the 1F entrance. Break the tree nuts for fairies to bottle.
  20. Optional · Treasure Chart 1
    After getting the Boomerang, return to the chest at the top of the second room. Clear the enemies around it with the Boomerang first.
    Get: Treasure Chart 1
  21. Boss · Boss: Kalle Demos
    See “Boss” below
    Get: Boss defeated, Heart Container
  22. Forest Haven · After clearing
    Rescue Makar, take the Heart Container and return to Forest Haven for Farore's Pearl.
    Get: Farore's Pearl

Boss: Kalle Demos (giant flower hanging from the ceiling)

  1. Its body hangs from the ceiling by vines, protected by closed petals.
  2. Cut the vines between the ceiling and the body with the Boomerang. Locking onto several at once is fastest.
  3. Once all the vines are cut, the body falls and the petals open; slash the weak spot in the center.
  4. Keep moving so the tentacles from the floor don't grab you while you aim at the vines.
