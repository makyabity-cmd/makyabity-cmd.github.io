Light Arrows

Ganon's Tower underground maze — Tower of the Gods (E4)

1) Defeat the four bosses in the central hall of Ganon's Tower to open the north door, then solve the candle puzzle. 2) In the middle room of the candle rooms, read the stone tablet; a hole opens in the floor—drop into the maze. 3) In the maze, Phantom Ganon drops a sword when defeated; the correct door is the one its hilt points to. With the fixed route, taking the way you face in the first room as forward: back → left → forward → left → right → forward. A wrong door leads to a room full of enemies. 4) Defeat Phantom Ganon in the last room and the chest gives you the Light Arrows (deflect his energy balls with your sword). 5) Go back to the first room through the far door, defeat Phantom Ganon again with a Light Arrow and hit the north wall with the black sword he leaves to continue. Note: the warp opened by the candle puzzle is just a shortcut back from the Forsaken Fortress waters if you leave the tower.

Late game

Requirements: Sail, Wind Waker, Place the 3 pearls on the Triangle Islands, Solve the candle puzzle in Ganon's Tower (opens the warp to the Forsaken Fortress)

Empty Bottle 4

Windfall Island — Windfall Island (D2)

The safe thief event. After Forsaken Fortress 2, at night, talk to Mila by the tree between the potion shop and the House of Wealth. When she sends you away, climb the nearby stairs and leave; she starts walking (just talking won't make her move). Follow her from far enough that she doesn't spot you when she turns around (if she does, try again the next night). When a rupee bubble appears over her head in front of the safe in Zunari's shop, talk to her from behind. Say you're a hero, hear her out and sympathize, but refuse to help her steal (“because I'm honest”) to get the bottle.

After Forsaken Fortress 2, at night

Requirements: Finish the first Forsaken Fortress and reach Windfall, Defeat the Helmaroc King (Forsaken Fortress 2), Song of Passing

Mother and Child Isles (B2)

Fire & Ice Arrows — Mother and Child Isles (B2)

You can only get inside the Mother and Child Isles by warping with the Ballad of Gales. The Queen of Fairies only appears after you rescue Aryll in Forsaken Fortress 2 (warping in earlier does nothing). You can't sail out, so play the Ballad of Gales on your boat to leave. Needed for Fire Mountain and Ice Ring Isle.

After Forsaken Fortress 2; Ballad of Gales

Requirements: Sail, Wind Waker, Defeat Kalle Demos (Forbidden Woods), Ballad of Gales, Defeat the Helmaroc King (Forsaken Fortress 2)
