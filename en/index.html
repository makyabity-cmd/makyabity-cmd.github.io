What you can do here

“Zelda Guides & Trackers” is an unofficial fan site for collecting everything in The Legend of Zelda games without missing a thing. For each game we cover where to find Pieces of Heart, Gold Skulltulas, Treasure Charts, figurines and more, and how to get them.

The main feature is the collection tracker each game has. Check off the items, songs and dungeons you've got, and it shows only what you can get with your current gear. Work through the list from the top and you'll cut down on backtracking and won't miss anything. Your checks are saved in your browser, so you can pick up where you left off any time.

New here?

  • Playing now: open the game's “Tracker”, check the items you have, then look at “Available now”.
  • Looking for one spot: from each game's “Guide” page you can reach the Piece of Heart and Gold Skulltula lists and the maps.
  • Ocarina of Time on Switch 2: many locations and contents changed from the N64 version, so use the Switch 2 pages.
