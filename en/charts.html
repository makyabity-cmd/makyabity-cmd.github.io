All 41 Treasure Charts in The Wind Waker

Where to get every Treasure Chart, where to salvage it and what is inside (GameCube / Switch 2, North American & European version). Sail to the square shown on the chart, line up the X with the islands around you and salvage with the Grappling Hook.

Treasure Chart 1 → Silver Rupee (200)

Get it: Forest Haven (F6) — Forbidden Woods 3F. Ride the Boko Baba bud up from 2F and defeat the enemies clinging to the chest with the Boomerang.

Salvage: Private Oasis (E5) waters

To get the chart: Sail, Wind Waker, Defeat Gohma (Dragon Roost Cavern), Deku Leaf, Boomerang

To salvage: Sail, Wind Waker, Defeat Kalle Demos (Forbidden Woods), Treasure Chart 1 (obtained), Grappling Hook

Treasure Chart 2 → Piece of Heart

Get it: Windfall Island (D2) — After Forsaken Fortress 2, give Maggie's father 20 Skull Necklaces.

Salvage: Rock Spire Isle (B3) waters

To get the chart: Finish the first Forsaken Fortress and reach Windfall, Defeat the Helmaroc King (Forsaken Fortress 2)

To salvage: Sail, Wind Waker, Defeat Kalle Demos (Forbidden Woods), Treasure Chart 2 (obtained), Grappling Hook

To salvage: Sail, Wind Waker, Defeat Kalle Demos (Forbidden Woods), Treasure Chart 3 (obtained), Grappling Hook
