Windfall errands

Rewards from Windfall's residents that aren't chests.

Advertisement

Become Lenzo's assistant (Joy Pendant)

Windfall Island (D2)

After getting the Picto Box, talk to Lenzo at his picto shop and become his assistant. Complete three tasks: 1) photograph Garrickson posting a letter, 2) photograph Gossack's face when he's scared in the café, 3) photograph Anton (out for a walk) and Linda (near the school) gazing at each other. Show all three for a Joy Pendant. Then show him a Forest Firefly in a bottle for the Deluxe Picto Box (on GameCube you can hold 3 pictos).

Picto Box

Requirements: Finish the first Forsaken Fortress and reach Windfall, Picto Box

Give Mrs. Marie one Joy Pendant (Red Rupee)

Windfall Island (D2)

After hearing about the Killer Bees, roll into the tree in front of the bomb shop and a Joy Pendant drops. Show and give it to Mrs. Marie at the school for a Red Rupee (20). This starts the pendant collection: 20 for the Cabana Deed, 40 in total for the Hero's Charm.

Requirements: Finish the first Forsaken Fortress and reach Windfall

Killer Bees hide-and-seek thanks (Purple Rupee)

Windfall Island (D2)

Given by Mrs. Marie at the school after you find all four Killer Bees (separate from the leader's Piece of Heart #19).

Requirements: Finish the first Forsaken Fortress and reach Windfall

Tingle's Chart from Tingle in jail

Windfall Island (D2)

A chart you get when you free Tingle from jail. Shows where Tingle Island is.

Requirements: Finish the first Forsaken Fortress and reach Windfall, Free Tingle from the Windfall jail

Auction (House of Wealth): Joy Pendant

Windfall Island (D2)

Auction item starting at 40 Rupees. Usually goes for around 150.

Song of Passing

Requirements: Finish the first Forsaken Fortress and reach Windfall, Song of Passing

Give the potion shop 15 Green Chu Jelly (Green Potion)

Windfall Island (D2)

Give Doc Bandam at the potion shop 15 Green Chu Jelly (dropped by green ChuChus) and he can make Green Potions (restore magic).

Spoils Bag

Requirements: Finish the first Forsaken Fortress and reach Windfall

Give the potion shop 15 Blue Chu Jelly (Blue Potion)

Windfall Island (D2)

Likewise, give him 15 Blue Chu Jelly and he can make Blue Potions (restore hearts and magic). Blue ChuChus are rare and only appear in a few places, so this takes a while.

Spoils Bag

Requirements: Finish the first Forsaken Fortress and reach Windfall

Decorate the gate with statues (Purple Rupee ×2)

Windfall Island (D2)

Give the statues from the trading quest to Kane by the town gate to decorate it; he thanks you with a Purple Rupee (twice).

After Forsaken Fortress 2; Delivery Bag

Requirements: Finish the first Forsaken Fortress and reach Windfall, Defeat the Helmaroc King (Forsaken Fortress 2), Delivery Bag

Complete the gate decorations (Red Rupee ×2)

Windfall Island (D2)

Keep decorating the gate with flags and other items from the trading quest; when a set is complete Kane gives you a Red Rupee (twice).

After Forsaken Fortress 2; Delivery Bag

Requirements: Finish the first Forsaken Fortress and reach Windfall, Defeat the Helmaroc King (Forsaken Fortress 2), Delivery Bag

Finish decorating the town (Piece of Heart)

Windfall Island (D2)

Once the gate decorations make the town lively, Sam on the bench thanks you with a Piece of Heart.

After Forsaken Fortress 2; Delivery Bag

Requirements: Finish the first Forsaken Fortress and reach Windfall, Defeat the Helmaroc King (Forsaken Fortress 2), Delivery Bag
