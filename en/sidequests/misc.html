Give Hoskit 20 Golden Feathers (Orange Rupee)

Dragon Roost Island (F2)

Give Hoskit on 2F of the Rito post office 20 Golden Feathers for an Orange Rupee on the spot. Later his girlfriend's letter brings Piece of Heart #35.

Spoils Bag

Requirements: Finish the first Forsaken Fortress and reach Windfall, Sail

Fire Mountain Big Octo (Orange Rupee)

Fire Mountain (F3)

Defeat the Big Octo in the Fire Mountain waters and salvage from the light ring that appears.

Grappling Hook, Boomerang or Hero's Bow

Requirements: Sail, Wind Waker, Defeat Gohma (Dragon Roost Cavern), Grappling Hook, Boomerang or Hero's Bow
