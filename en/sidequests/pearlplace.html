Placing the pearls

With all three pearls, place each one on the statue of the Northern, Eastern and Southern Triangle Islands. Once all three are placed, the Tower of the Gods appears (E4). Until all three are checked, the tower stays locked on the sea chart.

Place Din's Pearl on the Northern Triangle Island

Northern Triangle Island (D3)

Land on the island and place Din's Pearl at the statue.

Din's Pearl

Requirements: Sail, Wind Waker, Defeat Kalle Demos (Forbidden Woods), Din's Pearl

Place Farore's Pearl on the Eastern Triangle Island

Eastern Triangle Island (F4)

Land on the island and place Farore's Pearl at the statue.

Farore's Pearl

Requirements: Sail, Wind Waker, Defeat Gohma (Dragon Roost Cavern), Farore's Pearl

Place Nayru's Pearl on the Southern Triangle Island

Southern Triangle Island (D5)

Land on the island and place Nayru's Pearl at the statue.

Nayru's Pearl

Requirements: Sail, Wind Waker, Defeat Kalle Demos (Forbidden Woods), Nayru's Pearl
