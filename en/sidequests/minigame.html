Sploosh Kaboom (Battlesquid): Piece of Heart for the first win, Treasure Chart 7 for the second

Windfall Island (D2)

Run by Salvatore on Windfall (10 Rupees a try). Fire at the 8×8 grid and sink all the squids within 24 shots. First win: Piece of Heart; second win: Treasure Chart 7 (Purple Rupee after that). Squids lie straight, horizontally or vertically, without overlapping. After a hit, keep firing along that row or column. Sunk squids light up on the right, so you can avoid wasted shots.

10 Rupees

Requirements: Finish the first Forsaken Fortress and reach Windfall

Sploosh Kaboom (Battlesquid): Treasure Chart 23 in 19 shots or fewer

Windfall Island (D2)

Win in under 20 shots (19 or fewer) for Treasure Chart 23 (Orange Rupee after that).

10 Rupees

Requirements: Finish the first Forsaken Fortress and reach Windfall

Mail Sorting

Dragon Roost Island (F2)

At the Rito post office on Dragon Roost Island. In 30 seconds, throw each letter into the slot matching its stamp. The slots are fixed (top: red, yellow-green, yellow; bottom: purple, green, blue). Move the cursor to the next stamp while the letter is still flying. 25+ letters earn 3 Rupees each. Sorting 25+ after clearing Dragon Roost Cavern leads to Baito's event (Piece of Heart #34).

Free

Requirements: Finish the first Forsaken Fortress and reach Windfall, Sail, Wind Waker

Bird-Man Contest: Piece of Heart

Flight Control Platform (G2)

Flight Control Platform (10 Rupees). Glide as far as you can with the Deku Leaf. Set the wind northwest first and have your magic doubled by the Great Fairy at Two-Eye Reef, or you won't make it. Ride the first one or two updrafts, then fly straight. Save before trying.

10 Rupees, double magic

Requirements: Sail, Wind Waker, Defeat Kalle Demos (Forbidden Woods), Deku Leaf, Double magic, Wind's Requiem

Boating Course

Boating Course (F7)

Boating Course (30 Rupees). Collect the rupees on the barrels and reach the goal in time. Up to 150 Rupees. Jump over upright barrels (sideways ones you can pass). Watch for exploding barrels later on. Prioritize yellow and red rupees. The time limit drops by 10 seconds each time you clear it.

30 Rupees

Requirements: Sail, Wind Waker, Defeat Kalle Demos (Forbidden Woods)

Cannon game (Spectacle Island): Piece of Heart for the first win

Spectacle Island (C2)

Salvatore on Spectacle Island (50 Rupees). Destroy all five barrels with 10 shots. Line up left and right first, then adjust the range by the splashes (raise the angle if short, lower it if long).

50 Rupees

Requirements: Sail, Wind Waker, Defeat Kalle Demos (Forbidden Woods)

Cannon game (Spectacle Island): Treasure Chart 17 for the second win

Spectacle Island (C2)

From the third time on, 100 Rupees. Good for farming rupees once you get the hang of it.

50 Rupees

Requirements: Sail, Wind Waker, Defeat Kalle Demos (Forbidden Woods), Cannon game (Spectacle Island): Piece of Heart for the first win

Fishman target practice

Outset Island (B7)

After getting the Hero's Bow, scatter All-Purpose Bait for the Fishman in the sea near Outset Island and talk to him (hear about Outset from him first). Shoot him with 10 arrows as he leaps out of the sea. 10 Rupees per hit and a 100-Rupee bonus for all ten (200 total). Repeatable, so bring plenty of bait to farm rupees.

Hero's Bow, All-Purpose Bait

Requirements: Outset Island reachable (start of game, or after Forbidden Woods), Hero's Bow, Bait Bag

Orca's sword training

Outset Island (B7)

Once you can return to Outset Island (after the Forbidden Woods), talk to Orca at his house to train. Pass 500 hits to be called “trained” and get a Piece of Heart (#13).

Clear the Forbidden Woods

Requirements: Outset Island reachable (start of game, or after Forbidden Woods), Defeat Kalle Demos (Forbidden Woods)

Killer Bees hide-and-seek

Windfall Island (D2)

After talking to Mrs. Marie at the school on Windfall, find all four Killer Bees hiding around town and talk to them. The leader gives you a Piece of Heart (#19) and Mrs. Marie a Purple Rupee.

Requirements: Finish the first Forsaken Fortress and reach Windfall

Auction (House of Wealth)

Windfall Island (D2)

At the House of Wealth on Windfall (night only). Mash A to build up your bid. Typical prices: Piece of Heart (80 → about 230 Rupees), Treasure Chart 18 (6 → 130), Treasure Chart 38 (60 → 170), Joy Pendant (40 → 150). Bidding about 10% above the current price keeps others quiet for a while. Bid right at the 5-second signal to win. Press B to leave and re-enter if you don't want the item.

Rupees, Song of Passing

Requirements: Finish the first Forsaken Fortress and reach Windfall, Song of Passing
