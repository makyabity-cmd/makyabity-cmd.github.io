Awakening the sages

Medli must be awakened as a sage for the Earth Temple and Makar for the Wind Temple. Until these are checked, items inside those temples (Mirror Shield, Hookshot, Treasure Charts 5 and 35, Heart Containers) won't show as available.

Awaken Medli as the Sage of Earth

Dragon Roost Island (F2)

After learning the Earth God's Lyric in the Headstone Island cave, go to Dragon Roost Island. Exit through the back door on 2F of the Rito Aerie, go right, cross the gap with the Grappling Hook and climb the ladder. Raise the Wind Waker in front of Medli practicing her harp on the cliff and play the Earth God's Lyric. Then take Medli to the stone tablet on Headstone Island.

Earth God's Lyric, Grappling Hook

Requirements: Finish the first Forsaken Fortress and reach Windfall, Sail, Earth God's Lyric, Grappling Hook

Awaken Makar as the Sage of Wind

Forest Haven (F6)

After learning the Wind God's Aria in the Gale Isle cave, warp to Forest Haven with the Ballad of Gales. On the way to the Deku Tree, swing with the Grappling Hook from the branch toward the waterfall with music notes and jump into the cave behind it. At the bottom of the ladder, raise the Wind Waker in front of Makar playing his violin and play the Wind God's Aria. Then take Makar to the stone tablet on Gale Isle.

Wind God's Aria, Grappling Hook

Requirements: Sail, Wind Waker, Defeat Gohma (Dragon Roost Cavern), Wind God's Aria, Grappling Hook, Ballad of Gales
