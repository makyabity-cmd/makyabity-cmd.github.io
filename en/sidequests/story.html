Story milestones

Sea access is judged only from this section (bosses and so on), the sages, the pearls and your item checks. Defeating a boss and taking its Heart Container are separate flags, so this also works for no-container runs. Checking a Heart Container also checks the boss.

Defeat Gohma (Dragon Roost Cavern)

Dragon Roost Island (F2)

Check this when you beat the boss. Story progress uses this flag, so it also works for no-Heart-Container challenge runs.

Requirements: Finish the first Forsaken Fortress and reach Windfall, Sail

Defeat Kalle Demos (Forbidden Woods)

Forest Haven (F6)

Check this when you beat the boss. Story progress uses this flag, so it also works for no-Heart-Container challenge runs.

Requirements: Sail, Wind Waker, Defeat Gohma (Dragon Roost Cavern), Deku Leaf

Defeat Gohdan (Tower of the Gods)

Tower of the Gods (E4)

Check this when you beat the boss. Story progress uses this flag, so it also works for no-Heart-Container challenge runs.

Requirements: Sail, Wind Waker, Place the 3 pearls on the Triangle Islands, Bombs

Defeat the Helmaroc King (Forsaken Fortress 2)

Forsaken Fortress (A1)

Check this when you beat the boss. Story progress uses this flag, so it also works for no-Heart-Container challenge runs.

Requirements: Sail, Wind Waker, Master Sword, Bombs

Defeat Jalhalla (Earth Temple)

Headstone Island (C7)

Check this when you beat the boss. Story progress uses this flag, so it also works for no-Heart-Container challenge runs.

Requirements: Sail, Wind Waker, Defeat Kalle Demos (Forbidden Woods), Awaken Medli as the Sage of Earth

Defeat Molgera (Wind Temple)

Gale Isle (D1)

Check this when you beat the boss. Story progress uses this flag, so it also works for no-Heart-Container challenge runs.

Requirements: Sail, Wind Waker, Defeat Kalle Demos (Forbidden Woods), Awaken Makar as the Sage of Wind, Iron Boots, Skull Hammer

Finish the first Forsaken Fortress and reach Windfall

Outset Island (B7)

Ride Tetra's pirate ship from Outset to the Forsaken Fortress. When you reach Aryll's tower the Helmaroc King throws you to Windfall. Checking this opens Windfall and closes Outset until you clear the Forbidden Woods. It's also checked automatically when you check the Sail or the Wind Waker.

Requirements: Outset Island reachable (start of game, or after Forbidden Woods)

Complete the Triforce and return to the Tower of the Gods

Tower of the Gods (E4)

After completing the Triforce with all eight shards, sail to the Tower of the Gods and a ring of light appears on the sea. Enter it to reach Hyrule Castle. After the scene in the Master Sword chamber, defeat the two Darknuts and leave the castle; the way to Ganon's Tower opens.

All 8 Triforce Shards

Requirements: Sail, Wind Waker, Place the 3 pearls on the Triangle Islands, All 8 Triforce Shards

Solve the candle puzzle in Ganon's Tower (opens the warp to the Forsaken Fortress)

Tower of the Gods (E4)

Defeat the four bosses in the central hall of Ganon's Tower to open the north door. In the right room, count the candles on the wall (left to right: 2, 4, 1, 3). In the left room, hit the crystal switches with the Boomerang in order of fewest candles (2nd from right → far left → far right → 2nd from left). A warp opens in the waterway, linking to the Forsaken Fortress waters so you can sail back into the tower.

Boomerang

Requirements: Sail, Wind Waker, Place the 3 pearls on the Triangle Islands, Complete the Triforce and return to the Tower of the Gods, Boomerang

Defeat Ganondorf (final boss)

Tower of the Gods (E4)

After getting the Light Arrows, defeat Puppet Ganon beyond the big door in Ganon's Tower, climb the tower and defeat Ganondorf at the top. This triggers the ending. A Second Quest file is created afterwards, so save to a separate file if you want to keep playing your first file.

Light Arrows

Requirements: Sail, Wind Waker, Place the 3 pearls on the Triangle Islands, Light Arrows
