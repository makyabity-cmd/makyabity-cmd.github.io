Hurricane Spin (Knight's Crests)

Darknuts drop Knight's Crests when defeated (the Grappling Hook can't steal them). Bring 10 to Orca on Outset Island to learn the Hurricane Spin, which uses magic.

The Ice Ring Isle secret cave is recommended: frozen Darknuts appear and respawn when you leave and re-enter.
