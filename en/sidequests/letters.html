Letters (mailbox rewards)

Items enclosed in letters you can pick up at any mailbox. Letters containing Pieces of Heart (Komali's father, Baito's mother, Hoskit's girlfriend) and the IN-credible Chart have their own entries.

Advertisement

Letter from Orca (Red Rupee)

Windfall Island (D2)

Arrives after you clear the Forbidden Woods and see the Greatfish Isle event.

Requirements: Finish the first Forsaken Fortress and reach Windfall, Defeat Kalle Demos (Forbidden Woods)

Letter from Grandma (Red Rupee)

Windfall Island (D2)

Arrives some days after you cheer up Grandma on Outset Island by giving her a fairy in a bottle.

Empty Bottle, Song of Passing

Requirements: Finish the first Forsaken Fortress and reach Windfall, Empty Bottle, Song of Passing

Letter from Aryll (Red Rupee)

Windfall Island (D2)

Arrives some days after you rescue Aryll in Forsaken Fortress 2 and visit Hyrule Castle the second time.

Song of Passing

Requirements: Finish the first Forsaken Fortress and reach Windfall, Defeat the Helmaroc King (Forsaken Fortress 2), Song of Passing

Letter from Baito (Red Rupee)

Windfall Island (D2)

Take Baito's letter during the mail-sorting job, post it, and it arrives after you clear the Earth Temple (separate from his mother's letter with Piece of Heart #34).

Delivery Bag

Requirements: Finish the first Forsaken Fortress and reach Windfall, Defeat Jalhalla (Earth Temple), Delivery Bag

Beedle's shop ad letter (Beedle's Chart)

Windfall Island (D2)

Arrives after you get Bombs, announcing that Beedle's shop now sells them. Comes with a chart showing where the shop ships are.

Bombs

Requirements: Finish the first Forsaken Fortress and reach Windfall, Bombs

Rock Spire special shop ad letter (Green Rupee)

Windfall Island (D2)

Arrives after you upgrade your wallet, announcing Beedle's special shop on Rock Spire Isle.

Wallet upgrade

Requirements: Finish the first Forsaken Fortress and reach Windfall, Wallet upgrade

Beedle's Silver Membership reward

Windfall Island (D2)

Earn points by buying consumables (bait, bombs, arrows, potions) at Beedle's shops; you get this by letter when you reach Silver membership.

Song of Passing

Requirements: Finish the first Forsaken Fortress and reach Windfall, Song of Passing

Beedle's Gold Membership reward

Windfall Island (D2)

Earn even more points to reach Gold membership; this comes by letter.

Song of Passing

Requirements: Finish the first Forsaken Fortress and reach Windfall, Beedle's Silver Membership reward, Song of Passing
