Withered Trees (Forest Water)

Starts when you talk to the Deku Tree after clearing the Forbidden Woods. Fill a bottle with Forest Water from the stream inside Forest Haven and water all eight trees within 20 minutes of leaving the island to get a Piece of Heart (#39). If time runs out, the water turns ordinary and you must refill. The Ballad of Gales is essential. Check the time left in the item description or by talking to a Korok. The order below is one efficient route.

Preparation: draw Forest Water at Forest Haven

Forest Haven (F6)

Before going inside, set the wind east at the entrance to speed up the next trip.

Empty Bottle

Requirements: Sail, Wind Waker, Defeat Gohma (Dragon Roost Cavern), Defeat Kalle Demos (Forbidden Woods), Empty Bottle, Ballad of Gales

Cliff Plateau Isles (withered tree)

Cliff Plateau Isles (G6)

Land on the gentle slope and drop into the hole by the tree. Cross the log, defeat the Boko Baba and light the Boko Stick it drops at a torch. Carry it up with the Baba Bud, throw the burning stick at the distant wooden barricade (or use a Fire Arrow), glide across with the Deku Leaf and climb out where the light shines in.

Deku Leaf

Requirements: Sail, Wind Waker, Defeat Kalle Demos (Forbidden Woods), Preparation: draw Forest Water at Forest Haven, Ballad of Gales, Deku Leaf

Private Oasis (withered tree)

Private Oasis (E5)

Warp back to Forest Haven with the Ballad of Gales, set the wind northwest and sail there. The tree is near the beach.

Requirements: Sail, Wind Waker, Defeat Kalle Demos (Forbidden Woods), Preparation: draw Forest Water at Forest Haven, Ballad of Gales

Shark Island (withered tree)

Shark Island (C6)

Warp to Southern Fairy Island with the Ballad of Gales and head west to Shark Island. Land at the shark's tail.

Requirements: Sail, Wind Waker, Defeat Kalle Demos (Forbidden Woods), Preparation: draw Forest Water at Forest Haven, Ballad of Gales

Greatfish Isle (withered tree)

Greatfish Isle (B4)

Warp to Greatfish Isle with the Ballad of Gales. The tree is at the top of the southeast spiral slope.

Requirements: Sail, Wind Waker, Defeat Kalle Demos (Forbidden Woods), Preparation: draw Forest Water at Forest Haven, Ballad of Gales

Needle Rock Isle (withered tree)

Needle Rock Isle (A5)

Set the wind southwest and sail there. The tree is near the burning chest.

Requirements: Sail, Wind Waker, Defeat Kalle Demos (Forbidden Woods), Preparation: draw Forest Water at Forest Haven, Ballad of Gales

Eastern Fairy Island (withered tree)

Eastern Fairy Island (E3)

Warp to Dragon Roost Island with the Ballad of Gales and sail southwest to Eastern Fairy Island. The tree is at the island's edge.

Requirements: Sail, Wind Waker, Defeat Kalle Demos (Forbidden Woods), Preparation: draw Forest Water at Forest Haven, Ballad of Gales

Mother and Child Isles (withered tree)

Mother and Child Isles (B2)

Warp to Tingle Island with the Ballad of Gales, set the wind northwest and sail there. The tree is on the outer islet, so don't warp to the Mother and Child Isles directly (you'd land inside).

Requirements: Sail, Wind Waker, Defeat Kalle Demos (Forbidden Woods), Preparation: draw Forest Water at Forest Haven, Ballad of Gales

Star Island (withered tree)

Star Island (B1)

Head north to the tree in the middle of the island. Watch out for the shark (Gyorg) ramming you in the northern waters. The Piece of Heart falls after the eighth tree.

Requirements: Sail, Wind Waker, Defeat Kalle Demos (Forbidden Woods), Preparation: draw Forest Water at Forest Haven, Ballad of Gales
