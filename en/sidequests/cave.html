Secret caves (per chest)

Track each chest in the small caves you enter through holes on islands. With the Secret Cave Chart (salvaged with Treasure Chart 13), the sea chart shows which islands have caves. The Savage Labyrinth is on the Dungeons tab.

Advertisement

Star Island cave: Piece of Heart

Star Island (B1)

Blow up the boulder on the west side of the island to open the entrance. Defeat every enemy inside for the chest.

Bombs

Requirements: Sail, Wind Waker, Defeat Kalle Demos (Forbidden Woods), Bombs

Horseshoe Island cave: Treasure Chart 8

Horseshoe Island (A7)

Blow the island's seeds into the hole with the Deku Leaf to open the entrance. Defeat every enemy inside for the chest.

Deku Leaf

Requirements: Sail, Wind Waker, Defeat Kalle Demos (Forbidden Woods), Deku Leaf

Rock Spire Isle cave: Treasure Chart 37

Rock Spire Isle (B3)

Blow up the boulder up high to get in. Lighting the torches brings out Keese; defeat them all for the chest.

Bombs

Requirements: Sail, Wind Waker, Defeat Kalle Demos (Forbidden Woods), Bombs

Dragon Roost Island cave: Purple Rupee

Dragon Roost Island (F2)

Blow up the boulder near the Wind Shrine to get in. Defeat every enemy inside for the chest.

Bombs

Requirements: Finish the first Forsaken Fortress and reach Windfall, Sail, Bombs

Pawprint Isle (ChuChu cave): Piece of Heart

Pawprint Isle (E2)

Chest in the cave you reach by crawling under the central dome.

Crawl

Requirements: Finish the first Forsaken Fortress and reach Windfall, Sail

Pawprint Isle (ChuChu cave): Purple Rupee

Pawprint Isle (E2)

Chest behind the left boulder in the same cave (blow it up).

Bombs

Requirements: Finish the first Forsaken Fortress and reach Windfall, Sail, Bombs

Pawprint Isle (ChuChu cave): Joy Pendant

Pawprint Isle (E2)

Chest behind the right boulder in the same cave (blow it up).

Bombs

Requirements: Finish the first Forsaken Fortress and reach Windfall, Sail, Bombs

Pawprint Isle (ChuChu cave): Red Rupee

Pawprint Isle (E2)

Chest at the top of the climbable wall in the same cave.

None

Requirements: Finish the first Forsaken Fortress and reach Windfall, Sail

Pawprint Isle (Wizzrobe cave): Silver Rupee

Pawprint Isle (E2)

Hookshot up to the wooden statue on the island's “middle toe” to reach the entrance. Defeat all the Wizzrobes inside for the chest.

Hookshot

Requirements: Finish the first Forsaken Fortress and reach Windfall, Sail, Hookshot

Fire Mountain cave: Power Bracelets

Fire Mountain (F3)

Freeze the crater with an Ice Arrow (5 minutes) and drop in through the hole at the top. Defeat every enemy for the chest.

Ice Arrows

Requirements: Sail, Wind Waker, Defeat Gohma (Dragon Roost Cavern), Fire & Ice Arrows

Needle Rock Isle cave: Orange Rupee

Needle Rock Isle (A5)

Melt the chunk of ice on the edge of the island with a Fire Arrow to reveal a hole. Light all six torches inside with Fire Arrows for the chest.

Fire Arrows

Requirements: Sail, Wind Waker, Defeat Kalle Demos (Forbidden Woods), Fire & Ice Arrows

Ice Ring Isle cave: Iron Boots

Ice Ring Isle (E6)

Stop the cold for 5 minutes with a Fire Arrow, drop into the central hole and slide down the ice to the chest.

Fire Arrows

Requirements: Sail, Wind Waker, Defeat Kalle Demos (Forbidden Woods), Fire & Ice Arrows

Ice Ring Isle (inner cave): Orange Rupee

Ice Ring Isle (E6)

Walk through the strong wind on top of the island in the Iron Boots to the hole at the back. Thaw the frozen enemies with Fire Arrows and defeat them for the chest. Darknuts appear here, so it's good for farming Knight's Crests.

Iron Boots, Fire Arrows

Requirements: Sail, Wind Waker, Defeat Kalle Demos (Forbidden Woods), Iron Boots, Fire & Ice Arrows

Diamond Steppe Island (warp-pot maze): Joy Pendant

Diamond Steppe Island (A6)

Hookshot up the palm tree to the hole on the high ground. First chest of the warp-pot maze where you pick the right pots.

Hookshot

Requirements: Sail, Wind Waker, Defeat Kalle Demos (Forbidden Woods), Hookshot

Diamond Steppe Island (warp-pot maze): Ghost Ship Chart

Diamond Steppe Island (A6)

Last chest of the same maze.

Hookshot

Requirements: Sail, Wind Waker, Defeat Kalle Demos (Forbidden Woods), Hookshot

Shark Island cave: Silver Rupee

Shark Island (C6)

Activate the island's four switches to open the entrance (needs the Skull Hammer and Iron Boots). Defeat every enemy inside for the chest.

Skull Hammer, Iron Boots

Requirements: Sail, Wind Waker, Defeat Kalle Demos (Forbidden Woods), Skull Hammer, Iron Boots

Bomb Island cave: Piece of Heart

Bomb Island (F5)

Blow up the central boulder to get in. Roll up Magtails as weights on switches and put out the fires on your way to the chest.

Bombs

Requirements: Sail, Wind Waker, Defeat Kalle Demos (Forbidden Woods), Bombs

Angular Isles cave: Silver Rupee

Angular Isles (E7)

A puzzle with block piles and the Mirror Shield.

Mirror Shield

Requirements: Sail, Wind Waker, Defeat Kalle Demos (Forbidden Woods), Mirror Shield

Boating Course cave: Submarine Chart

Boating Course (F7)

Enter through the hole on the north island. Miniblins keep coming; defeat them with the Boomerang or bow for the chest.

Boomerang or Hero's Bow

Requirements: Sail, Wind Waker, Defeat Kalle Demos (Forbidden Woods), Boomerang or Hero's Bow

Cliff Plateau Isles cave: Joy Pendant

Cliff Plateau Isles (G6)

Chest in the cave you enter from the smaller island. The cave leads up onto the larger island (the Treasure Chart 25 chest up there is on the Treasure Charts tab).

Deku Leaf

Requirements: Sail, Wind Waker, Defeat Kalle Demos (Forbidden Woods), Deku Leaf

Stone Watcher Island cave: Triforce Chart 7

Stone Watcher Island (C5)

Lift the stone head with the Power Bracelets to get in. Clear the four rooms, defeat the Darknuts and play the Wind's Requiem.

Power Bracelets

Requirements: Sail, Wind Waker, Defeat Kalle Demos (Forbidden Woods), Power Bracelets, Wind's Requiem

Overlook Island cave: Triforce Chart 8

Overlook Island (G1)

Hookshot from the tree on the lowest islet up to the top to get in. Clear the four rooms and the Darknuts, then play the Wind's Requiem.

Hookshot

Requirements: Sail, Wind Waker, Defeat Kalle Demos (Forbidden Woods), Hookshot, Wind's Requiem

Bird's Peak Rock cave: Triforce Chart 3

Bird's Peak Rock (G5)

Use a Hyoi Pear to fly a seagull into the six switches in the nests; the entrance on the eastern islet opens. Play the Wind's Requiem inside.

Hyoi Pear

Requirements: Sail, Wind Waker, Defeat Kalle Demos (Forbidden Woods), Bait Bag, Wind's Requiem

Private Oasis cabana basement (upper floor): Triforce Chart 2

Private Oasis (E5)

Enter with the Cabana Deed and hang from the ceiling hook with the Grappling Hook to open a passage behind the fireplace. Chest on the upper floor.

Cabana Deed, Skull Hammer

Requirements: Sail, Wind Waker, Defeat Kalle Demos (Forbidden Woods), Cabana Deed (for Shard 2), Grappling Hook, Skull Hammer

Private Oasis cabana basement (lower floor): Orange Rupee

Private Oasis (E5)

Chest on the lower floor of the same basement.

Cabana Deed, Skull Hammer

Requirements: Sail, Wind Waker, Defeat Kalle Demos (Forbidden Woods), Cabana Deed (for Shard 2), Grappling Hook, Skull Hammer
