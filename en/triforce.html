Triforce Shards guide

How to collect all eight Triforce Shards in The Wind Waker (GameCube / Switch 2, NA/EU): get each Triforce Chart, have Tingle decipher it on Tingle Island (398 Rupees each), then salvage the shard.

Preparation

Free Tingle from the Windfall jail

You can do this as soon as you reach Windfall. Afterwards Tingle will decipher charts on Tingle Island (C3).

Requirements: Finish the first Forsaken Fortress and reach Windfall

Receive the IN-credible Chart

After defeating the Helmaroc King in Forsaken Fortress 2, check any mailbox: Tingle has sent you a cash-on-delivery letter (you must have freed him from jail). It costs 201 Rupees, more than the starting wallet holds, so upgrade your wallet at a Great Fairy first. The chart shows where every Triforce Chart is.

Requirements: Finish the first Forsaken Fortress and reach Windfall, Defeat the Helmaroc King (Forsaken Fortress 2), Wallet upgrade, Free Tingle from the Windfall jail

Cabana Deed (for Shard 2)

Give Mrs. Marie on Windfall 20 Joy Pendants. First finish the Killer Bees hide-and-seek and give her the Joy Pendant that drops from the tree in front of the school when you roll into it. The tracker doesn't count your pendants, so it only checks the Killer Bees event.

Requirements: Finish the first Forsaken Fortress and reach Windfall, Piece of Heart #19

Ghost Ship Chart (for Shard 4)

Hookshot onto Diamond Steppe Island, then pick the right warp pots in the cave on the high ground to reach the last chest. The Ghost Ship only appears at night and moves with the moon: full moon = Crescent Moon Island, new moon = Bomb Island, slightly waning (left) = Greatfish Isle, slightly waxing (right) = Diamond Steppe, crescent missing right = Spectacle Island, crescent missing left = Five-Star Isles, left half missing = Star Belt Archipelago.

Requirements: Sail, Wind Waker, Defeat Kalle Demos (Forbidden Woods), Hookshot

Shards 1–8

Triforce Shard 1

Chart: Islet of Steel (B5) — Islet of Steel. Destroy the cannons and gunboats with your ship's cannon or Bombs, break the gate and go inside. Play the Wind's Requiem on the blue floor to make a chest appear.

Salvage: Greatfish Isle (B4) waters

Bombs

To get the chart: Sail, Wind Waker, Defeat Kalle Demos (Forbidden Woods), Bombs

To decipher: Sail, Wind Waker, Defeat Kalle Demos (Forbidden Woods), Triforce Chart 1 (obtained), Free Tingle from the Windfall jail, Wallet upgrade

To salvage: Sail, Wind Waker, Defeat Kalle Demos (Forbidden Woods), Triforce Chart 1 (deciphered), Grappling Hook

Triforce Shard 2

Chart: Private Oasis (E5) — Under the cabana on the Private Oasis. Show the Cabana Deed to get in, hang from the ceiling hook with the Grappling Hook to reveal a passage behind the fireplace, crawl through, pound the two stakes with the Skull Hammer, defeat the two ReDeads and play the Wind's Requiem on the blue floor.

Salvage: Gale Isle (D1) waters

Cabana Deed, Skull Hammer

To get the chart: Sail, Wind Waker, Defeat Kalle Demos (Forbidden Woods), Cabana Deed (for Shard 2), Grappling Hook, Skull Hammer

To decipher: Sail, Wind Waker, Defeat Kalle Demos (Forbidden Woods), Triforce Chart 2 (obtained), Free Tingle from the Windfall jail, Wallet upgrade

To salvage: Sail, Wind Waker, Defeat Kalle Demos (Forbidden Woods), Triforce Chart 2 (deciphered), Grappling Hook

Triforce Shard 3

Chart: Bird's Peak Rock (G5) — Bird's Peak Rock. Use a Hyoi Pear to fly a seagull into all six switches in the nests on the tall rocks. The cave on the eastern islet opens; play the Wind's Requiem on the blue floor inside. Thin out the Kargarocs with arrows first.

Salvage: Stone Watcher Island (C5) waters

Hyoi Pear, Hero's Bow

To get the chart: Sail, Wind Waker, Defeat Kalle Demos (Forbidden Woods), Bait Bag, Wind's Requiem

To decipher: Sail, Wind Waker, Defeat Kalle Demos (Forbidden Woods), Triforce Chart 3 (obtained), Free Tingle from the Windfall jail, Wallet upgrade

To salvage: Sail, Wind Waker, Defeat Kalle Demos (Forbidden Woods), Triforce Chart 3 (deciphered), Grappling Hook

Triforce Shard 4

Chart: Diamond Steppe Island (A6) — Inside the Ghost Ship. With the Ghost Ship Chart (Diamond Steppe Island), sail into the Ghost Ship at night where the moon phase says it will be, defeat all enemies and open the chest at the back.

Salvage: Outset Island (B7) waters

Ghost Ship Chart, Song of Passing

To get the chart: Sail, Wind Waker, Defeat Kalle Demos (Forbidden Woods), Ghost Ship Chart (for Shard 4), Song of Passing

To decipher: Sail, Wind Waker, Defeat Kalle Demos (Forbidden Woods), Triforce Chart 4 (obtained), Free Tingle from the Windfall jail, Wallet upgrade

To salvage: Outset Island reachable (start of game, or after Forbidden Woods), Triforce Chart 4 (deciphered), Grappling Hook

Triforce Shard 5

Chart: Needle Rock Isle (A5) — Defeat the golden gunboat near Needle Rock Isle to get Triforce Chart 5.

Salvage: Cliff Plateau Isles (G6) waters

Bombs

To get the chart: Sail, Wind Waker, Defeat Kalle Demos (Forbidden Woods), Bombs

To decipher: Sail, Wind Waker, Defeat Kalle Demos (Forbidden Woods), Triforce Chart 5 (obtained), Free Tingle from the Windfall jail, Wallet upgrade

To salvage: Sail, Wind Waker, Defeat Kalle Demos (Forbidden Woods), Triforce Chart 5 (deciphered), Grappling Hook

Triforce Shard 6

Chart: Outset Island (B7) — Savage Labyrinth B30 on Outset Island. Hookshot up the tree on the cliff behind Link's house, lift the boulder with the Power Bracelets and go in. Clear each floor down to B30 and play the Wind's Requiem on the blue floor.

Salvage: Southern Triangle Island (D5) waters

Hookshot, Power Bracelets

To get the chart: Outset Island reachable (start of game, or after Forbidden Woods), Hookshot, Power Bracelets, Wind's Requiem

To decipher: Sail, Wind Waker, Defeat Kalle Demos (Forbidden Woods), Triforce Chart 6 (obtained), Free Tingle from the Windfall jail, Wallet upgrade

To salvage: Sail, Wind Waker, Defeat Kalle Demos (Forbidden Woods), Triforce Chart 6 (deciphered), Grappling Hook

Triforce Shard 7

Chart: Stone Watcher Island (C5) — Cave under the Stone Watcher Island. Lift the stone head with the Power Bracelets and drop in. Clear the four rooms, defeat the Darknuts in the center and play the Wind's Requiem on the blue floor.

Salvage: Seven-Star Isles (F1) waters

Power Bracelets

To get the chart: Sail, Wind Waker, Defeat Kalle Demos (Forbidden Woods), Power Bracelets, Wind's Requiem

To decipher: Sail, Wind Waker, Defeat Kalle Demos (Forbidden Woods), Triforce Chart 7 (obtained), Free Tingle from the Windfall jail, Wallet upgrade

To salvage: Sail, Wind Waker, Defeat Kalle Demos (Forbidden Woods), Triforce Chart 7 (deciphered), Grappling Hook

Triforce Shard 8

Chart: Overlook Island (G1) — Top of Overlook Island. Hookshot your way up starting from the tree on the lowest islet. Clear the four rooms, defeat the Darknuts and play the Wind's Requiem on the blue floor.

Salvage: Two-Eye Reef (D7) waters

Hookshot

To get the chart: Sail, Wind Waker, Defeat Kalle Demos (Forbidden Woods), Hookshot, Wind's Requiem

To decipher: Sail, Wind Waker, Defeat Kalle Demos (Forbidden Woods), Triforce Chart 8 (obtained), Free Tingle from the Windfall jail, Wallet upgrade

To salvage: Sail, Wind Waker, Defeat Kalle Demos (Forbidden Woods), Triforce Chart 8 (deciphered), Grappling Hook
