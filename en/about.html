About this site and disclaimer

“Zelda Guides & Trackers” is an unofficial fan site run by an individual, with guides and collection trackers for The Legend of Zelda series. It is not affiliated with Nintendo in any way. “The Legend of Zelda” is a trademark of Nintendo.

Games covered

  • The Legend of Zelda: The Wind Waker (GameCube, Wii U and Switch 2): islands, dungeons, Pieces of Heart, Treasure Charts and a collection tracker
  • The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time (Nintendo 64 and 3DS): maps of every Piece of Heart, Gold Skulltula and chest, dungeon walkthroughs and a collection tracker
  • The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time (Nintendo Switch 2): Switch 2 Pieces of Heart, Gold Skulltulas and maps, plus a tracker that takes you to the end of the game

What makes this site different

Every game has a collection tracker you check off as you play. It narrows the list down to what you can get right now based on your items, songs and events, so working from the top lets you finish the game without missing anything. The maps were redrawn from scratch by this site based on the in-game map screens.

About the information

No game images, map screenshots or audio are used. The guide information was compiled independently by the site owner from their own playthroughs and publicly available material.
