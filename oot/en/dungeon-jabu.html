Inside Jabu-Jabu's Belly

Nintendo 64Nintendo 3DS
This guide covers the Nintendo 64 and 3DS (Ocarina of Time 3D) versions. Chests, Pieces of Heart and Gold Skulltulas are in the same places in the N64, GameCube and 3DS versions. The Switch 2 version is still being researched.

Walkthrough

This is the N64 route. We will update it for the Switch 2 version as we research.

  1. Give Lord Jabu-Jabu a fish from a bottle to get inside.
  2. You meet Princess Ruto. Carry her through the dungeon and set her on switches to work the mechanisms.
  3. Defeat the school of Stingers to get the Boomerang.
  4. Use the Boomerang to defeat the three tentacles (red, blue, green), picking up the Dungeon Map and Compass.
  5. Defeat the mini-boss Bigocto to continue.
  6. Barinade: cut the roots holding it to the ceiling with the Boomerang, clear the jellyfish, then attack the body. You get a Heart Container and Zora's Sapphire.
