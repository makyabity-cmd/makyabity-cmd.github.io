Dodongo's Cavern

Nintendo 64Nintendo 3DS
This guide covers the Nintendo 64 and 3DS (Ocarina of Time 3D) versions. Chests, Pieces of Heart and Gold Skulltulas are in the same places in the N64, GameCube and 3DS versions. The Switch 2 version is still being researched.

Walkthrough

This is the N64 route. We will update it for the Switch 2 version as we research.

  1. Blow up the boulder at the entrance with a Bomb Flower (Goron's Bracelet).
  2. In the main hall, blast the muddy wall with a Bomb Flower for the Dungeon Map.
  3. Head through the east rooms; the Compass is in the room guarded by an Armos statue.
  4. After the second Lizalfos fight you get the Bomb Bag, so you can use your own bombs from here.
  5. From the upper bridge, drop bombs into both eyes of the giant Dodongo skull to open its mouth.
  6. Inside the mouth, fight King Dodongo: feed him a bomb when he opens his mouth, then slash him while he is down. You get a Heart Container and Goron's Ruby.
