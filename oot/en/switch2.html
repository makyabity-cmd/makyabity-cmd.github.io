Ocarina of Time on Nintendo Switch 2

Nintendo Switch 2
A Nintendo Switch 2 exclusive released on November 5, 2026. It's a full remake based on the N64 version, with rebuilt graphics and controls and fully voiced cutscenes.

Compiled from several guide sites published after release and checked in our own playthrough.

New in the Switch 2 version

  • Hall of Fallen Foes: a dream world you can visit after the Forest Temple by talking to Sheik in the Temple of Time and sleeping in a bed. Check statues of defeated monsters for info, and rematch bosses to record clear times.
  • Fairy Thoughts: pale glowing patterns around the world. Navi reads them aloud, giving little stories and adventure hints. One of the Hyrule Field Pieces of Heart involves them.
  • Business Scrub Seed: a new item that calls a Business Scrub to where you are. You can buy potions and Deku Nuts even in dungeons. If you don't buy anything, you get the seed back.
  • Chapter recap: from the − Button menu, see a summary of each chapter and your next goal. As the story progresses the tapestry fills in and you can look back on scenes.
  • ZELDA NOTES: works with the Nintendo Switch App on your phone. Play records, “rumor quests” where you find places from photos, quizzes and more.
  • amiibo: Zelda series amiibo give items that help your adventure. The Young Link and Young Zelda amiibo (due in 2027) give special masks.

Control changes

  • Move the camera freely with the right stick. Targeting is on ZL (hold or toggle, set in options).
  • No more automatic jumping off ledges; you jump with a button whenever you like (the jump button can be changed in options).
  • Hold the button after a roll to dash. It also speeds you up while swimming.
  • Hold L or R and use the stick or D-pad to switch items without opening the menu.
  • The first-person view for the bow and Slingshot is gone; aim with motion controls.
  • With the ocarina set to “Humming” or “Instrument” in options, you can play it with sound into the microphone.

Main gameplay differences

  • Time passes wherever you are (even in Castle Town it goes from day to dusk to night). You can enter and leave Castle Town at night.
  • Night doesn't come until you get Zelda's Letter at Hyrule Castle. Child night-only events and Gold Skulltulas come after that.
  • You can hold up to 200 Rupees from the start with the Kokiri wallet.
  • The trick for multiplying bugs in a bottle doesn't work; catch bugs each time you release them on soil.
  • The white dog event happens only after you show Zelda's Letter to the Death Mountain guard.
  • You resume from where you saved. There's one save file, with autosave.
  • In hot places like Death Mountain Crater you take damage instead of having a time limit.
  • The Hookshot only sticks to set spots.
  • You pick up Pieces of Heart with the A Button. You can't grab them from a distance with the Boomerang or similar.
  • The Stone of Agony's rumbling is gone, and hidden holes are opened by blowing up rocks. The 20-Skulltula reward is the Stone of Agony.
  • Opening a Great Fairy Fountain costs Rupees.
  • Masks can be used as an adult too. The Bunny Hood reward is 200 Rupees.
  • The Spin Attack doesn't use magic. Rupees dropped by enemies don't disappear over time.
  • The Fairy Ocarina stays in your collection even after you get the Ocarina of Time.
  • You can't change Link's name.

Main event and dungeon differences

  • Malon gives you the egg as soon as you reach the castle, and it hatches without waiting for morning.
  • In the Lost Woods session with the Skull Kids, the notes to play are shown on screen.
  • Dampé's gravedigging: talk to him first and he digs along the way.
  • The Zora diving game has you collect 5 scales instead of Rupees.
  • Big Poes appear when you jump a fence with Epona. Sell 5 to the Poe Collector in the guardhouse at the Castle Town entrance for an empty bottle, and 10 for a Gold Rupee (200 Rupees).
  • The Bottom of the Well has different chest contents and counts (more Rupees; 4 chests removed and 2 added). One Small Key comes from a coffin you open by draining the water and lighting it with a Deku Stick.
  • In the Fire Temple, the Dungeon Map, Compass and one Small Key have swapped places; the bomb chest is now a Red Rupee, and the huge Rupee spot is now 3 chests (2 Red Rupees and a Purple Rupee).
  • Horseback archery at Gerudo's Fortress is daytime only. You get the quiver upgrade at 1400 points or more.
  • The Lake Hylia Fishing Pond costs 30 Rupees. You get no prizes if you use the sinking lure.
  • In the Forest Temple, key usage changed; for example, you need a Small Key to go from the entrance room into the central hall (reflected in the tracker).
  • The Running Man gives you a Gold Rupee. You can talk to him in the daytime too.
  • On the way to deliver the Eye Drops, you need to break a rock in Death Mountain Crater with the Megaton Hammer.
  • The Graveyard Piece of Heart grave moved two to the left, and you no longer need the Sun's Song.
  • The Forest Temple slide puzzle has no time limit and is now about completing a picture. A lift was added in the northwest room.
  • After the Water Temple, the ice in Zora's Domain melts and you can warp back from Lake Hylia.
  • Fire Temple lava floors sink as soon as you step on them. For frozen eye switches, melt the ice with a Fire Arrow, then hit them again with an arrow.
  • Some map and dungeon layouts changed, such as more torches inside the Deku Tree.

Hidden holes with no checks (notes)

Hidden holes left out of the tracker because they have no chest.

  • Lost Woods hidden hole: a cave full of grass that always gives bugs. No chest. Handy for collecting bugs to release on soil.
  • Hidden hole in the east of Hyrule Field: a cave with Rupees lying around and no chest.
  • Hidden hole in the west of Hyrule Field (near Gerudo Valley): burn the three webs with Din's Fire to find pots with Rupees, a cow and a Gossip Stone.
