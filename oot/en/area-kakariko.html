Kakariko Village

Nintendo 64Nintendo 3DS
This guide covers the Nintendo 64 and 3DS (Ocarina of Time 3D) versions. Chests, Pieces of Heart and Gold Skulltulas are in the same places in the N64, GameCube and 3DS versions. The Switch 2 version is still being researched.

A simple map drawn from the game's collision and placement data (north is up). Squares are chests, S circles are Gold Skulltulas, ♥ are Pieces of Heart and E diamonds are events; tap one to jump to it below. Things inside buildings are placed at the entrance, and things from people at that person. Some, such as grottos, are not on the map.

Bugs (for soft-soil Skulltulas)

There are 1 bug spot(s) here (“B” on the map). How to get and reuse bugs

Rounding up the Cuccos (Bottle)

As a child, talk to the Cucco lady (Anju) near the village entrance. She asks you to put her seven Cuccos back in the pen. Pick each Cucco up, carry it back and throw it over the fence. Bring all seven back for a Bottle.

Where the Cuccos are (from the game's placement data; C1–C7 on the map):

  • C1：west of House of Skulltula
  • C2：near Potion Shop, on a raised ledge
  • C3：east of Impa's House
  • C4：north east of Windmill, on a raised ledge
  • C5：north of House
  • C6：north of House of Skulltula
  • C7：east of Granny's Potion Shop

Tip: jump while holding a Cucco to glide. For Cuccos on high ledges, glide from the ledge toward the pen. If you drop one outside, just pick it up again.
