All 36 Pieces of Heart (Switch 2)

Nintendo Switch 2
This page covers the Nintendo Switch 2 version. For the N64 / 3DS versions, see here.

Compiled from several guide sites published after release and checked in our own playthrough.

Every four pieces add one heart. There are 36 pieces, the same as the N64 version, but in the Switch 2 version the Ice Cavern piece is gone and there are new spots in Death Mountain Crater and Hyrule Field. You now pick up Pieces of Heart with the A Button, so you can no longer grab them from a distance with the Boomerang or Hookshot. Pieces you have collected are also shown on the in-game map.
