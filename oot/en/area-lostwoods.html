Lost Woods

Nintendo 64Nintendo 3DS
This guide covers the Nintendo 64 and 3DS (Ocarina of Time 3D) versions. Chests, Pieces of Heart and Gold Skulltulas are in the same places in the N64, GameCube and 3DS versions. The Switch 2 version is still being researched.

Time
Time is stopped (stays as when you entered) (Day and night)
Getting there as a child
Nothing special
Getting there as an adult
Nothing special (after drawing the Master Sword)

Simple map

A simple map drawn from the game's collision and placement data (north is up). Squares are chests, S circles are Gold Skulltulas, ♥ are Pieces of Heart and E diamonds are events; tap one to jump to it below. Things inside buildings are placed at the entrance, and things from people at that person. Some, such as grottos, are not on the map.
