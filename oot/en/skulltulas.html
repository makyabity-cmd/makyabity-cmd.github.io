All 100 Gold Skulltulas

Nintendo 64Nintendo 3DS
This guide covers the Nintendo 64 and 3DS (Ocarina of Time 3D) versions. Chests, Pieces of Heart and Gold Skulltulas are in the same places in the N64, GameCube and 3DS versions. The Switch 2 version is still being researched.

Getting and reusing bugs (for soft-soil Gold Skulltulas)

Gold Skulltulas in soft soil come out when you release bugs on the soil. You need an empty bottle to carry bugs.

  1. Find: bugs come out from specific rocks you lift or grass you cut. Spots set to release bugs in the game data are marked “Bug” on each area map (Kakariko Village, the graveyard, Zora's River, Lake Hylia, Gerudo Valley, the Lost Woods, the Desert Colossus, Hyrule Castle, Death Mountain Trail and Goron City).
  2. Catch: swing an empty bottle at the bugs.
  3. Buy: the Kakariko potion shop sells bottled bugs (50 Rupees).
  4. Release: use the bugs on soft soil; they burrow in and the Skulltula appears.
  5. Reuse: bugs that do not burrow can be caught again, so one bottle covers several soil patches.

Tip: there are no bug spots near the soft soil in Kokiri Forest, so bring bugs from elsewhere once you can leave the forest. In the Lost Woods, there is bug grass near the soft soil.

Many only appear at night. Release bugs from a bottle on soft soil to make one come out.
