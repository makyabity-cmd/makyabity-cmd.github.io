Day and night

Nintendo 64Nintendo 3DS
This guide covers the Nintendo 64 and 3DS (Ocarina of Time 3D) versions. Chests, Pieces of Heart and Gold Skulltulas are in the same places in the N64, GameCube and 3DS versions. The Switch 2 version is still being researched.

Time does not pass everywhere. The game's map data gives each area a time speed, and time only moves in seven areas.

Where time passes

Passes
Hyrule Field, Zora's River, Lake Hylia, Gerudo Valley, Desert Colossus, Hyrule Castle, Death Mountain Trail
Stopped
Kokiri Forest, Lost Woods, Sacred Forest Meadow, Kakariko Village, Kakariko Graveyard, Lon Lon Ranch, Hyrule Castle Town Market, Temple of Time, Zora's Domain, Zora's Fountain, Goron City, Death Mountain Crater, Gerudo's Fortress, Haunted Wasteland

In areas where time is stopped, the time stays as it was when you entered. If you wait for night in Hyrule Field and then enter Kakariko Village, it stays night there. Time also does not pass in dungeons or indoors.

Outside Ganon's Castle, the time is fixed at 6:30 p.m.

How to make it night

  1. Wait in an area where time passes (such as Hyrule Field) until night, then go where you need to
  2. Play the Sun's Song to switch between day and night

At the start, child Link cannot leave Kokiri Forest, and time is stopped there, so it never becomes night. Night-only things are out of reach until Saria gives you the Fairy Ocarina at the forest exit after you save the Deku Tree, or until you learn the Sun's Song. The tracker's “Available now” uses this rule.

Day only

To make it day, wait for morning where time passes or play the Sun's Song.

In the game data, the Market shooting gallery, Happy Mask Shop, potion shop and bazaar close their doors at night, and the Kakariko shooting gallery is closed at night for adult Link (the Treasure Chest Shop is the opposite: night only). Borrowing masks therefore also has to be done during the day. The castle courtyard where you meet Zelda can only be reached during the day; at night the guards' layout changes and there is no way in.

  • Hyrule Castle: Get Zelda's Letter from Zelda in the courtyard
  • Hyrule Castle Town Market: After selling all four masks, borrow the Mask of Truth at the Happy Mask Shop
  • Hyrule Castle Town Market: Bigger Seed Bag at the Market shooting gallery
  • Lon Lon Ranch: Win the race against Ingo to get Epona. Go to the ranch in the daytime and pay Ingo 10 Rupees to ride. Call Epona with Epona's Song, mount her and talk to Ingo to race (50 Rupees). Win twice; he locks the gate, so jump the fence with Epona to escape
  • Kakariko Village: Bigger Quiver at the Kakariko shooting gallery
  • Hyrule Castle: Learn Zelda's Lullaby from Impa after meeting Zelda
  • Lon Lon Ranch: Learn Epona's Song from Malon
  • Kakariko Village: Borrow the Keaton Mask and sell it to the gate guard
  • Lost Woods: Borrow the Skull Mask and sell it to the Skull Kid on the stump
  • Kakariko Graveyard: Borrow the Spooky Mask and sell it to the boy in the graveyard (day)
  • Kakariko Village: Borrow the Pocket Egg from Anju
