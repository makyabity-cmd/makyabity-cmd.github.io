Songs

Nintendo 64Nintendo 3DS
This guide covers the Nintendo 64 and 3DS (Ocarina of Time 3D) versions. Chests, Pieces of Heart and Gold Skulltulas are in the same places in the N64, GameCube and 3DS versions. The Switch 2 version is still being researched.

There are 13 songs you can play on the ocarina. Six of them are warp songs.

Zelda's Lullaby

Taught by Impa after you meet Zelda in the castle courtyard

Effect: Opens things marked with the Triforce symbol

Child

Epona's Song

Taught by Malon at Lon Lon Ranch

Effect: Calls Epona. Cows give you milk when they hear it

Child

Saria's Song

Taught by Saria at the end of Sacred Forest Meadow

Effect: Lets you talk to Saria

Child

Sun's Song

From the stone tablet deep inside the Royal Family's Tomb

Effect: Switches between day and night. Freezes ReDeads

Child

Song of Time

Taught by Zelda when you get the Ocarina of Time

Effect: Moves Song of Time blocks

Child

Song of Storms

Hear it from the man in the windmill as an adult, then play it to him as a child

Effect: Brings rain and can open hidden holes

Child

Scarecrow's Song

Teach a tune to the scarecrow at Lake Hylia as a child, then play it to him as an adult

Effect: Calls Pierre the scarecrow as a Hookshot target

Adult

Minuet of Forest

Taught by Sheik in Sacred Forest Meadow

Effect: Warps to Sacred Forest Meadow

Adult

Bolero of Fire

Taught by Sheik at the entrance to Death Mountain Crater

Effect: Warps to Death Mountain Crater

Adult

Serenade of Water

Taught by Sheik at the exit of the Ice Cavern

Effect: Warps to Lake Hylia

Adult

Requiem of Spirit

Taught by Sheik when you reach the Desert Colossus as an adult

Effect: Warps to the Desert Colossus

Adult

Nocturne of Shadow

Taught by Sheik in Kakariko Village after you have the Forest, Fire and Water Medallions

Effect: Warps to Kakariko Graveyard

Adult

Prelude of Light

Taught by Sheik in the Temple of Time after the Forest Temple

Effect: Warps to the Temple of Time

Adult
