All 100 Gold Skulltulas (Switch 2)

Nintendo Switch 2
This page covers the Nintendo Switch 2 version. For the N64 / 3DS versions, see here.

Compiled from several guide sites published after release and checked in our own playthrough.

There are 100, the same as the N64 version, but in the Switch 2 version some have moved, a few into different areas. Many of the ones outdoors only appear at night (you can make it night with the Sun's Song). The number found in each area is also shown on the in-game map.

In the Switch 2 version the trick for multiplying bugs in a bottle doesn't work. You need to catch bugs each time you release them on soft soil.

Tokens and rewards

TokensReward
10Adult's Wallet (holds up to 500 Rupees)
20Stone of Agony (reacts to nearby Gold Skulltulas)
30Giant's Wallet (holds up to 1000 Rupees)
4020 Bombchus
50Piece of Heart
100200 Rupees (repeatable)

Collect the rewards at the House of Skulltula in Kakariko Village. In the Switch 2 version the 20-token reward is the Stone of Agony (reacts to nearby Gold Skulltulas), and the wallet sizes have changed.
