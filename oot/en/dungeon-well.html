Bottom of the Well

Nintendo 64Nintendo 3DS
This guide covers the Nintendo 64 and 3DS (Ocarina of Time 3D) versions. Chests, Pieces of Heart and Gold Skulltulas are in the same places in the N64, GameCube and 3DS versions. The Switch 2 version is still being researched.

Age
Child
How to get there
The well in Kakariko Village. Play the Song of Storms in the windmill as a child to drain it

Walkthrough

This is the N64 route. We will update it for the Switch 2 version as we research.

  1. As a child, play the Song of Storms in the windmill to drain the well, then go in.
  2. There are many fake walls and hidden holes. Explore what you can, then defeat Dead Hand underground for the Lens of Truth.
  3. Use the Lens to find invisible paths and fake walls and collect the remaining chests.
