Crossing to the far side (fortress side)

As a child the bridge is intact, but as an adult it is broken, so you cannot simply walk to the far side (the carpenters' tent and Gerudo's Fortress). There are two ways across:

  • Ride Epona and jump the gap from the near side of the broken bridge
  • Use the Longshot on the post on the far side (the Hookshot does not reach)

Until you have Epona or the Longshot you cannot reach the Skulltulas by the tent and rock arch, the carpenter boss, or anything beyond Gerudo's Fortress. After you rescue all four carpenters, the bridge is repaired and you can walk across.
