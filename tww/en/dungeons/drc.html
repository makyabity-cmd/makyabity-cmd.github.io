Dragon Roost Cavern walkthrough

The first dungeon, inside the mountain on Dragon Roost Island. You climb through 1F–3F and outdoor paths, rescue Medli and get the Grappling Hook, then go back down for the Big Key. The puzzles revolve around lighting Boko Sticks to burn torches and wooden barricades, and throwing water pots on lava to cool it into platforms.

Location: Dragon Roost Island (F2) · Story chapter 4

How to enter: After the Rito Chieftain's scene on Dragon Roost Island, give Komali his father's letter, then talk to Medli on the rocks outside; she throws you up the cliff to the entrance.

Chests & items (20)

  1. 1F · Small Key
    Defeat the two Bokoblins in the first room, light the Boko Stick they drop at the torch in the back right, then light the two unlit torches in the back left to make a chest appear.
  2. 1F · Dungeon Map
    Lava room. Throw the large green-patterned pot (full of water) onto the lava to cool it into a platform and reach the chest.
  3. 1F · Joy Pendant (water jug)
    In the water jug on the high shelf in the Dungeon Map room. Roll into the wall under the shelf to knock it down.
  4. 1F · Small Key
    Past the map room: defeat the Moblin and break the wooden barricades with your sword (lower left of the map).
  5. 2F · Compass
    Back inside, pull a block out of the wall to climb to 2F, then use a block as a step again; it's right beside it.
  6. 2F · Small Key
    Light a Boko Stick from a pot at a torch, throw it at the wooden fence on the south side of the upper level to burn it, and take the ladder to the south platform.
  7. 2F · Small Key
    In the Kargaroc nest at the top of the broken stairs, after turning left (not a chest).
  8. 2F · Joy Pendant
    Dark room past the locked door. Chest behind the barricade you burn with a Boko Stick.
  9. 2F · Treasure Chart 39
    Room with three Bokoblins. Carry fire from the lower torch to the unlit torch on the upper level with a Boko Stick to make a chest appear.
  10. 2F · Joy Pendant (pot)
    Pot on the high shelf in the same room. Roll into the wall to knock it down.
  11. 4F · Grappling Hook
    After the mini-boss (two Bokoblins, then a Moblin), Medli gives it to you once she's out of the cage.
  12. 3F · Knight's Crest
    Room before the boss room. Chest you reach with the Grappling Hook.
  13. 3F · Yellow Rupee
    Chest on the left side of the room before the boss room (opposite the Knight's Crest chest).
  14. 3F · Joy Pendant (pot)
    In the pot on the right side of the room before the boss room.
  15. 3F→2F · Joy Pendant
    In the 3F right room, defeat the Bokoblin on the rope bridge and a chest appears on the floor below. Cut the bridge ropes to drop down and get it.
  16. 1F · Big Key
    Magtail room. Hit its eye when its jaws open to make it curl up, then carry it onto the switch to put out the flames around the chest.
  17. 1F · Treasure Chart 11
    Room west of the central lava room. Cross the lava with the Grappling Hook and break the barricade (come back after getting the hook).
  18. Boss · Boss defeated
    Defeat Gohma. Can be checked separately from the Heart Container.
  19. 3F · Heart Container
    Defeat the boss, Gohma.
  20. Dragon Roost Island · Din's Pearl
    After clearing the dungeon, Komali gives it to you by the pond on Dragon Roost Island.

Walkthrough

  1. 1F · Move the statues at the entrance
    Of the three statues in front, pull the left one toward you, then pull the middle one left through the gap to reveal a passage.
  2. 1F · Light the torches for a Small Key
    Defeat the two Bokoblins, pick up a Boko Stick, carry the torch flame to the two unlit torches.
    Get: Small Key
  3. 1F · Through the central lava room from the left
    Where the bridge is broken, drop down, pull a block out of the wall to use as a step and jump. Throw the Bomb Flower past the rope bridge at the boulder to blow it up.
  4. 1F · Make a platform with a water pot and get the map
    Throw the large green-patterned pot onto the lava and it hardens into a platform. Make another with a pot near the chest and head to the south ladder.
    Get: Dungeon Map, Joy Pendant (water jug)
  5. 1F · Small Key past the Moblin
    Defeat the Moblin and break the wooden barricades blocking the passage with your sword.
    Get: Small Key
  6. 1F · Back to the central room to blow up the boulder
    Throw a nearby rock at the Bomb Flower growing on the wall to destroy the boulder. Open the left door with the Small Key.
  7. 1F · Burn the barricade and go outside
    Light the Bokoblins' Boko Sticks at the torch and burn the wooden barricade. The floor switch at the back opens the gate and leads outside.
  8. Outside · Climb the outdoor path
    Cross the rope bridge and climb the ladder (lava erupts, so move when it stops). Shimmy along the narrow ledge on the left. Before the boulder, hang from the wooden ledge on the left and move along it, then throw a Bomb Flower from the upper north side at the boulder to reveal a door.
  9. 2F · Use a block to reach 2F; Compass
    Pull a block out of the wall to reach 2F, pull out another and climb; the Compass is right beside it.
    Get: Compass
  10. 2F · Throw a burning stick to burn the fence
    Get a Boko Stick from the pots on the north platform, light it and throw it from the edge at the wooden fence to the south. Cross to the south by ladder for the Small Key, then go back and open the door.
    Get: Small Key
  11. 2F · Key from the Kargaroc nest
    Climb the broken stairs, defeat the Kargaroc and take the Small Key from its nest. Go to the locked door.
    Get: Small Key
  12. 2F · Through the dark room
    Light a Boko Stick and burn the barricade (the chest beside it has a pendant), then light the torch by the gate to open it. In the next room, blow up the rock on the pot by the entrance with a Bomb Flower to open the warp pot.
    Get: Joy Pendant, d_tingle
  13. 2F · Defeat the three Bokoblins
    Crossing the rope bridge locks you in. One is in the room; the other two hide in a pot on 1F and a pot on the high shelf on 2F. Carry the torch fire to the upper level for Treasure Chart 39.
    Get: Treasure Chart 39, Joy Pendant (pot)
  14. 2F→3F · Ride the erupting lava up to 3F
    Hit a Magtail with a water pot, or attack its eye when its jaws open, to make it curl up. While the lava is still, throw a water pot onto the vent to make a platform and ride the eruption up to 3F.
  15. 3F · Break the rocks and continue
    Out the door, use the Bomb Flower at the lower right to break the left rock (breaking the right rock opens a warp pot to the entrance).
  16. 3F→4F · Run up the crumbling stairs to the mini-boss
    The outer stairs crumble over time, so hurry. In the open area, defeat two Bokoblins and then the Moblin that drops from above.
  17. 4F · Grappling Hook from Medli
    Talk to Medli once she's out of the cage and she gives you the Grappling Hook.
    Get: Grappling Hook
  18. 4F→3F · Grapple to the room before the boss room
    On the stairs beside the open area, hook the branch and swing across. Grapple over the next drop, break the fence with your sword and go down (the room before the boss room).
    Get: Knight's Crest, Yellow Rupee, Joy Pendant (pot)
  19. 3F · Grapple to the 3F right room
    Hook the bars on the left and swing across three times. Defeat the Bokoblin on the rope bridge and a chest appears on the floor below.
    Get: d_tstatue
  20. 3F→1F · Down by the rope bridge and cage
    Cut the rope bridge to drop to 2F and grab the chest. Jump onto the hanging cage past the door and cut its rope to reach 1F.
    Get: Joy Pendant
  21. 1F · Hang from the ceiling bar to open the gate
    From the east platform cross the hanging platform to the ladder. Hook the short bar overhead; your weight works the mechanism and opens the gate.
  22. 1F · Turn on the rope to swing across
    After hooking the bar, hold R to stop swinging, turn right with the Control Stick, then swing again and jump to the platform. Use the bar past the hanging platform to reach the east door.
  23. 1F · Magtail on the switch for the Big Key
    Attack a Magtail's eye to curl it up, carry it and set it on the switch; the flames around the chest go out.
    Get: Big Key
  24. 1F · (Optional) Treasure Chart 11
    With the Grappling Hook, cross the lava in the room west of the central lava room and break the barricade for Treasure Chart 11.
    Get: Treasure Chart 11
  25. 3F · To the boss room
    The warp pot is the quickest way back. In the room before the boss, hook the ceiling bar to reach the north platform. The two small pots at the very top contain fairies (catch one in a bottle).
  26. 3F · Boss: Gohma
    See “Boss” below
    Get: Boss defeated, Heart Container
  27. Dragon Roost Island · After clearing
    Komali gives you Din's Pearl by the pond on Dragon Roost Island. From now on, packages arrive by mail. Talk to the King of Red Lions on the southwest shore and set sail with the wind blowing south.
    Get: Din's Pearl

Boss: Gohma (giant armored beast)

  1. Your sword can't crack its shell. Grab Valoo's tail hanging from the ceiling with the Grappling Hook.
  2. The shaking drops a rock from the ceiling onto Gohma and cracks its shell. Do this three times to break the shell.
  3. Once the shell is gone, aim for its weak spot, the eye. Pull the eye toward you with the Grappling Hook, then hit it with your sword.
  4. Keep your distance and circle around to avoid Gohma's charges and pincers.
