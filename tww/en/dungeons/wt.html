Wind Temple walkthrough

Explored together with Makar, awakened as the Sage of Wind. You resist strong winds with the Iron Boots and move upward on updrafts with the Deku Leaf. Partway through Makar is kidnapped; you get the Hookshot and go back to rescue him.

Location: Gale Isle (D1) · Story chapter 12

How to enter: Get the Iron Boots on Ice Ring Isle and learn the Wind God's Aria on Gale Isle. Warp to Forest Haven with the Ballad of Gales and, on the way to the Deku Tree, swing with the Grappling Hook from the branch toward the waterfall with music notes and jump into the cave behind it. At the bottom of the ladder, play the Wind God's Aria in front of Makar practicing his violin to awaken him as the Sage of Wind. Makar waits on the King of Red Lions; return to Gale Isle (north of Windfall) and play the Wind God's Aria together at the stone tablet in the cave to open the entrance.

Puzzle tips

  • Press R to have Makar follow you or wait. Control him with the Command Melody; press A on soft soil to grow a tree (a Hookshot target).
  • Use the Iron Boots to resist strong winds, bounce high off springs and break cracked floors.
  • You often glide on updrafts with the Deku Leaf. Cut grass to refill your magic.
  • Play the Wind God's Aria with Makar at stone tablets.

Walkthrough

  1. Preparation · Before entering the temple
    1) Shoot a Fire Arrow at Ice Ring Isle to stop the cold (5 minutes), drop into the central hole and slide down the ice for the Iron Boots (if you fall, grapple the pillar). 2) On Gale Isle, push through the wind in the Iron Boots, smash the wind-spewing rock with the Skull Hammer and enter the cave; learn the Wind God's Aria at the tablet. 3) Warp to Forest Haven, swing with the Grappling Hook toward the waterfall with music notes into the cave behind it, and play the Wind God's Aria for Makar at the bottom of the ladder. 4) Makar waits on the boat; return to Gale Isle and play the Wind God's Aria together at the tablet.
    Get: Iron Boots, Wind God's Aria
  2. 1F · Grow trees to stop the wind
    Defeat the enemies and use the Command Melody to have Makar grow trees in all the soft soil for 100 Rupees. Press the switch on the high ground at the back to stop the wind. Bounce off the spring in the Iron Boots, take them off midair and glide across with the Deku Leaf.
    Get: 100 Rupees
  3. 1F · Windmills and spinning blades
    Turn the windmills with the Deku Leaf to make a path and time your way past the spinning blades. Have Makar plant a tree in the soft soil to open the door.
  4. 1F · Makar is kidnapped
    Planting trees in all the soil in the next area opens the door, but a Floormaster carries Makar away. Continue to the right room.
  5. 1F · Up with the springs
    Go down, turn the windmill with the Deku Leaf to advance, then turn the wall back with the Deku Leaf. Step on the spring in the Iron Boots to go up. The right chest has a pendant.
    Get: Joy Pendant, wt_tstatue
  6. 1F · Map in the updraft room
    Ride the updraft with the Deku Leaf to the switch at the back left. Land on the platform using the updrafts and get the map from the chest on the south side. Enemies knock you down, so defeat them first. Take the left door.
    Get: Dungeon Map
  7. 1F→B1F · Drop down with the floor
    Step on the big room's switch in the Iron Boots and the floor drops to B1F. Break the lid of the pot right beside it to make a warp pot linked to the entrance. Compass in the chest at the back left.
    Get: Compass
  8. B1F · Small Key and Treasure Chart 35 in the south room
    Break the cracked floor with the Iron Boots to go down. Push the block with a spring onto the different-colored tile, put the other block beside it and bounce up for the Small Key. Break all the cracked floors to open five holes for Treasure Chart 35.
    Get: Small Key, Treasure Chart 35
  9. B1F · Mini-boss: Wizzrobe → Hookshot
    Open the north door with the Small Key. See “Mini-boss” below. Defeat it for the Hookshot.
    Get: Hookshot
  10. B1F→1F · Hookshot to Makar
    Hookshot the target on the front wall, pound the switch with the Skull Hammer and go to the previous room. Hookshot up to the upper floor and glide west from the east platform. Use the springs and Hookshot to reach where Makar is held.
    Get: Joy Pendant
  11. 1F · Rescue Makar
    Fire the Hookshot at the statue blocking the cell exit while wearing the Iron Boots to break it. The chest there has a pendant.
    Get: Joy Pendant
  12. 1F · Climb to the upper room using trees
    Return to the 1F upper left room and Hookshot up the tree Makar planted (fly Makar up with the Command Melody). Stop the Bubbles in the next room with the Deku Leaf or Ice Arrows and defeat them. After the enemies, plant a tree in the soil, carry Makar up, then Hookshot up yourself.
  13. 2F · Two-person switch, then the updraft south
    Press the switch together with Makar to open the way down; carry Makar and jump. Planting a tree in the soil creates an updraft; glide with the Deku Leaf to the 2F south door (the chest on the way has a pendant). Bring Makar along on the updraft.
    Get: Joy Pendant, Joy Pendant
  14. 2F · Defeat the Armos for a Small Key
    Defeat all the Armos in the south room for the chest.
    Get: Small Key
  15. 1F · Defeat the Darknuts for the Big Key
    Go right on 1F. Play the Wind God's Aria with Makar at the tablet to continue. Separate the Darknuts so you fight them one on one and finish them with the parry attack.
    Get: Big Key
  16. B1F · Treasure Chart 5 east of the fan
    Drop to B1F and go under the giant fan. Open the east (right) door with the Small Key to find a tall room. To reach the upper ledges, break the wall statue with the Hookshot while wearing the Iron Boots, then climb with the Hookshot and Deku Leaf. Defeat every Stalfos, Wizzrobe, Morth and Bokoblin in the room for the Treasure Chart 5 chest (pull the upper Bokoblins down with the Hookshot).
    Get: Treasure Chart 5
  17. B1F · Send Makar around the wind
    Control Makar to fly around the windy area from above and plant a tree in the soft soil. Link then Hookshots over to the tree.
  18. B1F · Strong-wind room
    Stop the spinning blades with boxes: lock the near blade to one side with the first, push the second into the groove in the Iron Boots to form a bridge, and stop the far blade with the third. Carry Makar to the back in the Iron Boots and play the Wind God's Aria at the tablet to reveal the boss door.
  19. B1F · Boss: Molgera
    See “Boss” below
    Get: Boss defeated, Heart Container
  20. After clearing · On to the Triforce
    Take the Heart Container. The Master Sword regains its true power. Next, gather the Triforce Shards.

Mini-boss: Wizzrobe (orange cape)

  1. Until you defeat the orange Wizzrobe, it keeps summoning minions.
  2. It often stays up high, so target it and shoot with the Hero's Bow.
  3. It only appears here and can't be fought again, so take its picto if you want the figurine.

Boss: Molgera (giant creature in the sand)

  1. When it surfaces and sticks out its tongue, Hookshot the tip of the tongue to pull it in, then slash it.
  2. Defeat the small creatures that appear as you fight.
  3. Later it leaps out and dives at you; run sideways to dodge, then aim for the tongue the same way.
