All 44 Pieces of Heart in The Wind Waker

Where to find every Piece of Heart and Heart Container in The Legend of Zelda: The Wind Waker (GameCube / Switch 2, North American & European version), with the items you need for each. Four pieces make one heart; all 44 pieces plus 6 Heart Containers give you 20 hearts.

Pieces of Heart (44)

Forsaken Fortress (A1)

In the jail cell on 1F. Step on the floor switch in the corner of the 1F room directly below the Dungeon Map room (where the barrels are) to open the cell. Easier on your second visit, when you have a sword.

Easier on 2nd visit

Requirements: Sail, Wind Waker, Master Sword

Star Island (B1)

Move the big boulder and defeat every enemy in the cave below; a chest appears.

BombsPower Bracelets

Requirements: Sail, Wind Waker, Defeat Kalle Demos (Forbidden Woods), Bombs
