Trading quest

Starts when you talk to Zunari on Windfall after getting the Delivery Bag on Dragon Roost Island. Up to three trades per day, each with a fee (760 Rupees in total in the order below). Play the Song of Passing twice to advance a day. The Goron merchants are on Greatfish Isle (B4), the Mother and Child Isles (B2) and Bomb Island (F5).
