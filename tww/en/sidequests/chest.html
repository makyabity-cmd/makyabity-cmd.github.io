Island & sea chests

Chests on islands, lookout platforms, submarines and in houses. Chests that are the same as entries on other tabs (Pieces of Heart, Treasure Charts) are linked and shown under those entries on the sea chart.

Advertisement

Outset Island: chest in Mesa's house (Red Rupee)

Outset Island (B7)

Chest in Mesa's house by the field, where he cuts the grass

Requirements: Outset Island reachable (start of game, or after Forbidden Woods)

Windfall Island: chest at the back of the jail (Picto Box)

Windfall Island (D2)

Chest past the maze at the back of the jail where Tingle is locked up

Requirements: Finish the first Forsaken Fortress and reach Windfall

Windfall Island: left chest on 2F of Lenzo's house (Treasure Chart 29)

Windfall Island (D2)

You can't reach the 2F back room from inside; enter through the outside balcony door. Glide with the Deku Leaf from the wooden stairs of the House of Wealth (or the Ferris wheel) to the balcony, adjusting the wind. Crawl through the passage at the back to see Lenzo and Minenco talking on 1F (take the picto for Treasure Chart 24 here).

Deku Leaf

Requirements: Finish the first Forsaken Fortress and reach Windfall, Deku Leaf

Windfall Island: right chest on 2F of Lenzo's house (Purple Rupee)

Windfall Island (D2)

Same room as the left chest. Enter from the outside balcony with the Deku Leaf.

Deku Leaf

Requirements: Finish the first Forsaken Fortress and reach Windfall, Deku Leaf

Windfall Island: chest in the House of Wealth (Red Rupee)

Windfall Island (D2)

Chest inside the House of Wealth, where the night auction is held

Requirements: Finish the first Forsaken Fortress and reach Windfall

Windfall Island: transparent chest revealed by the lighthouse (Piece of Heart)

Windfall Island (D2)

Once you light the lighthouse lamp with a Fire Arrow at night (the Piece of Heart #22 event), a transparent chest appears high up in the beam at night only. Glide over with the Deku Leaf or use the Hookshot. It's invisible during the day, so make it night with the Song of Passing first.

Fire Arrows, Wind's Requiem, Song of Passing, Deku Leaf or Hookshot

Requirements: Finish the first Forsaken Fortress and reach Windfall, Fire & Ice Arrows, Wind's Requiem, Song of Passing, Deku Leaf or Hookshot

Dragon Roost Island: chest on the big boulder (Silver Rupee)

Dragon Roost Island (F2)

Chest on top of the island's big boulder. Blow up the Bomb Flowers around it with the bow, Boomerang, a seagull controlled by a Hyoi Pear, or your boat's cannon to make a path.

Bow, Boomerang, seagull or cannon

Requirements: Finish the first Forsaken Fortress and reach Windfall, Sail, Hero's Bow or Boomerang or Bait Bag or Bombs

Dragon Roost Island: chest past the platforms around the island (Orange Rupee)

Dragon Roost Island (F2)

Chest at the end of the platforms around the island; glide from one to the next with the Deku Leaf, setting the wind.

Deku Leaf, Wind's Requiem

Requirements: Finish the first Forsaken Fortress and reach Windfall, Sail, Deku Leaf, Wind's Requiem

Forest Haven: chest on the southeast islet (Treasure Chart 3)

Forest Haven (F6)

From the west side of the island, set the wind southeast and glide to the islet with the Deku Leaf.

Deku Leaf, Wind's Requiem

Requirements: Sail, Wind Waker, Defeat Gohma (Dragon Roost Cavern), Deku Leaf, Wind's Requiem

Greatfish Isle: chest in the cliff ledge (Piece of Heart)

Greatfish Isle (B4)

Same chest as Piece of Heart #10

Deku Leaf, Wind's Requiem

Requirements: Sail, Wind Waker, Defeat Kalle Demos (Forbidden Woods), Deku Leaf, Wind's Requiem

Ice Ring Isle: frozen chest (Treasure Chart 36)

Ice Ring Isle (E6)

Melt the frozen chest on the edge of the island with a Fire Arrow.

Fire Arrows

Requirements: Sail, Wind Waker, Defeat Kalle Demos (Forbidden Woods), Fire & Ice Arrows

Private Oasis: chest above the waterfall (Treasure Chart 27)

Private Oasis (E5)

Hookshot or Deku Leaf

Requirements: Sail, Wind Waker, Defeat Kalle Demos (Forbidden Woods), Hookshot or Deku Leaf
